SmartStop Self Storage REIT, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

LADERA RANCH, CA – November 4, 2026 – SmartStop Self Storage REIT, Inc. (“SmartStop” or “the Company”), a self-managed and fully-integrated self storage company, announced its overall results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

 

Three Months Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Highlights:

•
Net income attributable to common stockholders was approximately $9.0 million. This represents an increase of approximately $3.8 million when compared to the same period in 2025. Net income per share of Common Stock, (basic and diluted) was $0.16. This represents an increase of approximately $0.07 per share when compared to the same period in 2025.
•
Total self storage-related revenues were approximately $68.2 million, an increase of approximately $3.6 million when compared to the same period in 2025.
•
FFO, as adjusted (attributable to common stockholders and Operating Partnership (“OP”) unit holders), was approximately $30.3 million, an increase of approximately $2.9 million when compared to the same period in 2025.
•
FFO, as adjusted per share and OP unit outstanding – diluted was $0.51, an increase of approximately $0.04 per share when compared to the same period in 2025.
•
Same-store revenues increased by 2.0%, same-store property operating expenses decreased by 3.8%, and same-store net operating income (“NOI”) increased by 4.9% compared to the same period in 2025.
•
On a constant currency basis for SmartStop’s Canadian properties included in its wholly-owned same-store pool, its aggregate same-store revenues for all properties included in the pool increased by 2.1%, same-store expenses decreased by 3.6%, and same-store NOI increased by 5.1% compared to the same period in 2025.
•
Same-store average physical occupancy was 92.2%, a decrease of approximately 0.4% compared to the same period in 2025.
•
Same-store annualized rent per occupied square foot was approximately $20.99, an increase of approximately 2.6% when compared to the same period in 2025.

 

Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Highlights:

•
Net income attributable to common stockholders was approximately $29.8 million. This represents an increase of approximately $41.4 million when compared to the same period in 2025. Net income per share of Common Stock, (basic and diluted) was $0.53. This represents an increase of approximately $0.80 per share when compared to the same period in 2025.
•
Total self storage-related revenues were approximately $198.8 million, an increase of approximately $14.2 million when compared to the same period in 2025.
•
FFO, as adjusted (attributable to common stockholders and OP unit holders), was approximately $88.4 million, an increase of approximately $25.4 million when compared to the same period in 2025.
•
FFO, as adjusted per share and OP unit outstanding – diluted was $1.49, an increase of approximately $0.18 per share when compared to the same period in 2025.

 

 

•
Same-store revenues increased by 1.6%, same-store property operating expenses decreased by 2.2%, and same-store NOI increased by 3.6% compared to the same period in 2025.
•
On a constant currency basis for SmartStop’s Canadian properties included in its wholly-owned same-store pool, its aggregate same-store revenues for all properties included in the pool increased by 1.5%, same-store expenses decreased by 2.3%, and same-store NOI increased by 3.5% compared to the same period in 2025.
•
Same-store average physical occupancy was 92.3%, a decrease of approximately 0.3% compared to the same period in 2025.
•
Same-store annualized rent per occupied square foot was approximately $20.48, an increase of approximately 1.9% when compared to the same period in 2025.

 

“SmartStop delivered another strong quarter, with sector-leading same-store NOI growth of 4.9% and FFO as adjusted per share growth of over 9%, even with a currency headwind,” said H. Michael Schwartz, Founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of SmartStop. “2.0% same-store revenue growth and a 3.8% reduction in same-store operating expenses expanded our operating margin by 190 basis points, our third consecutive quarter of margin improvement. These results allowed us to raise our full-year same-store NOI guidance for the fourth time this year.”

“We also put the Deca Initiative to work,” continued Mr. Schwartz. “We are expanding our Canadian platform by over 30% through our pending investment in Strategic Storage Canada, launched a programmatic investment partnership, and issued a $200 million CAD Maple Bond at attractive long term pricing, all while reducing our leverage. We entered the fourth quarter with momentum and remain focused on compounding long-term value per share for our shareholders.”

Acquisitions

SmartStop is under contract to acquire two stabilized properties in Las Vegas, Nevada, and Asheville, North Carolina, for approximately $37 million, comprising more than 186,000 net rentable square feet and approximately 1,600 units. These acquisitions are expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026.

Strategic Asset Management Program

During the quarter, SmartStop initiated a strategic asset management program to pursue potential opportunistic dispositions of selected wholly-owned properties located in non-core markets, initially targeting $75 million to $125 million of property sales beginning in early 2027. These non-core markets are generally markets in which SmartStop owns only a single or handful of properties and where management believes the Company is unlikely to achieve the scale necessary to realize meaningful operating efficiencies or clustering benefits. SmartStop intends to use net proceeds from these dispositions to recycle capital into its core markets, where greater density allows SmartStop to leverage shared on-site staffing, marketing spend, and revenue management across multiple properties.

Strategic Storage Canada Joint Venture

On September 29, 2026, SmartStop agreed to an investment of approximately $74.3 million CAD (approximately $52.3 million USD as of September 30, 2026) into a Canadian joint venture fund (“Strategic Storage Canada”), subject to various closing and other conditions. The investment will provide SmartStop with a 50% general partner interest in the fund and an immediate expected approximate 34% limited partnership interest in the fund that SmartStop expects to own at close, or shortly thereafter, 14 self storage properties in Canada, comprising approximately 961,000 net rentable square feet and 9,600 units. The portfolio is in early lease-up and is approximately 50% physically occupied. The investment is structured as approximately (i) $49.3 million CAD of limited partnership interests and (ii) $25.0 million CAD of convertible preferred partnership interests bearing a 6.5% rate to be paid quarterly in-kind, convertible (at the election of the other shareholders of the general partner) to limited partnership interests in stages over the 24 months following the closing at the net asset value of the fund established at close, which, if converted, would provide an ultimate expected limited partner ownership of approximately 44% at the end of year two. SmartStop also committed to make additional investments of up to $228.0 million CAD (approximately $160.5 million USD as of September 30, 2026) during the three years following the closing, primarily to fund additional self storage properties that may be acquired by the fund, all subject to the approval of the general partner of the fund. Within that amount, it is contemplated that approximately $5.0 million CAD may be requested for working capital and other permitted general purposes of the fund, and up to approximately $10.0 million CAD may be requested in connection with refinancings of fund indebtedness and other long-term items. Any such additional investment, including those working capital and refinancing amounts, requires general partner approval, which requires SmartStop’s separate

Additionally, SmartStop expects to provide guarantees directly to certain lenders of the fund’s subsidiaries, proportionate to its common ownership interest in the fund.

The investment is expected to position SmartStop as Canada’s third-largest self-storage operator, with 70 operating properties, expanding SmartStop’s presence in the existing markets of Vancouver, British Columbia, Calgary, Alberta, and the Greater Toronto Area, and establishing a new presence in the markets of Halifax, Nova Scotia and Quebec City, Quebec. Strategic Storage Canada will also serve as a Canadian-domiciled vehicle, which may be utilized for future contributions from Canadian self-storage owners in a tax-efficient manner.

As of September 30, 2026, SmartStop began property managing all 17 of the properties related to the transaction. Upon closing the investment, 14 of those assets (those expected to be owned by Strategic Storage Canada), will be taken out of the Third-Party Management property count, and instead be included in the Joint Venture Owned property count.

The investment and related transactions pertaining to this closing are subject to approval under the Canadian Competition Act and certain other closing conditions customary in transactions of this nature. Due to these closing conditions, SmartStop cannot make any assurances that the investment and all of the related transactions pertaining to this closing will occur. SmartStop anticipates completing the transaction in the fourth quarter of 2026.

As of September 30, 2026, SmartStop had not made any investment or deposit, nor had it provided any guarantees, related to this potential joint venture investment.

Bridge Lending and Preferred Investment Platform

During the quarter, the Company closed on two preferred investments with our joint venture partner AXCS Capital totaling $18.2 million. Additionally, the Company closed one bridge loan totaling $16.7 million. The weighted average yield between the investments is approximately 17.6%, before accounting for AXCS Capital’s management fee and promoted interest.

In October, SmartStop entered into a $10.0 million CAD (approximately $7.0 million USD as of September 30, 2026) delayed draw loan facility (the “Canadian Portfolio Delayed Draw Loan”) to provide capital for two self storage projects in Ontario, Canada. The loan bears interest at a fixed rate of 8.5% and matures on October 9, 2029. On October 9, 2026, SmartStop advanced $0.8 million CAD under the Canadian Portfolio Delayed Draw Loan.

Financing Activities

During the quarter, SmartStop closed a $200 million CAD (approximately $140.8 million USD as of September 30, 2026) aggregate principal amount of Series C senior unsecured notes, due February 18, 2031 (the “2031 Canadian Notes”). The 2031 Canadian Notes were issued by SmartStop’s operating partnership, SmartStop OP, L.P. The 2031 Canadian Notes bear interest at a rate of approximately 4.32% per annum, payable in cash in equal semiannual installments commencing on February 18, 2027. The 2031 Canadian Notes are rated BBB with a Stable Outlook by Morningstar DBRS and BBB with a Stable Outlook by Kroll Bond Rating Agency.

During the quarter, SmartStop utilized its at-the-market equity program to sell approximately 2.4 million shares on a forward basis at an initial gross average price of $32.01 per share for gross proceeds of up to approximately $78.1 million. SmartStop anticipates settling the forward shares in the fourth quarter of 2026, consistent with the timing of the above investment activity.

Managed REIT Platform Update

As of September 30, 2026, SmartStop, through an indirect subsidiary, served as the sponsor of Strategic Storage Growth Trust III, Inc. (“SSGT III”), Strategic Storage Trust VI, Inc. (“SST VI”), and Strategic Storage Trust X (“SST X” together with SSGT III and SST VI, the “Managed REITs”). Subsequent to September 30, 2026, SST VI acquired SSGT III, as described below. SmartStop receives asset management fees, property management fees, acquisition fees, and other fees, as applicable and receives substantially all of the tenant protection program revenue earned by the Managed REITs, which had a combined portfolio of 52 operating properties and approximately 43,000 units and 4.6 million rentable square feet at quarter end. Assets under management for the Managed REITs was approximately $1.0 billion at quarter end.

Effective on November 1, 2026, and pursuant to a definitive Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “SST VI-SSGT III Merger Agreement”), SST VI acquired SSGT III by way of a merger of SSGT III with and into a wholly-owned subsidiary of SST VI, with such subsidiary surviving (the “SST VI-SSGT III Merger”). As a result of the SST VI-SSGT III Merger, each outstanding share of SSGT III common stock or limited partnership interest was converted into the right to receive one share of SST VI common stock or one limited partnership interest in SST VI, as applicable.

As a result of the SST VI-SSGT III Merger, SST VI acquired or assumed all of the real estate, other assets, debt and other liabilities of SSGT III. The assets became subject to the SST VI Advisory Agreement, whose terms are substantially the same as those of the SSGT III Advisory Agreement. The SSGT III Advisory Agreement terminated immediately prior to the closing of the SST VI-SSGT III Merger, as described below, and no further fees will be paid thereunder. Following the SST VI-SSGT III Merger, SmartStop is no longer entitled to receive a subordinated distribution pursuant to the special limited partnership interest it held in SSGT III OP. However, the former SSGT III assets are now subject to the similar special limited partnership interest SmartStop holds in SST VI OP.

Concurrently with the entry into the SST VI-SSGT III Merger Agreement, SSGT III, SSGT III OP, and the SSGT III advisor entered into a termination agreement with respect to the SSGT III Advisory Agreement. Under that agreement, the SSGT III Advisory Agreement terminated immediately prior to the closing of the SST VI-SSGT III Merger. Notwithstanding the disposition fee and related calculations under the SSGT III Advisory Agreement, the termination agreement fixed the amount payable to the SSGT III advisor immediately prior to closing at $2.0 million, which was paid in units of limited partnership interest in SSGT III OP. Pursuant to certain post-merger assignments, these units were exchanged into an equivalent amount of limited partnership interests in SST VI OP.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION REGARDING NOI, FFO, and FFO, as adjusted

NOI

NOI is a non-GAAP measure that SmartStop defines as net income (loss), computed in accordance with GAAP, generated from properties, before corporate general and administrative expenses, asset management fees, interest expense, depreciation, amortization, acquisition expenses, tenant protection economics, stock compensation related to SmartStop’s IPO Grant and other non-property related income and expense. SmartStop believes that NOI is useful for investors as it provides a measure of the operating performance of its operating assets because NOI excludes certain items that are not associated with the ongoing operation of the properties. Additionally, SmartStop believes that NOI is a widely accepted measure of comparative operating performance in the real estate community. However, SmartStop’s use of the term NOI may not be comparable to that of other real estate companies as they may have different methodologies for computing this amount. In addition, NOI is not a substitute for net income (loss), cash flows from operations, or other related financial measures, in evaluating SmartStop’s operating performance.

Funds from Operations (“FFO”) and FFO, as Adjusted

FFO

FFO is a non-GAAP financial metric promulgated by NAREIT that SmartStop believes is an appropriate supplemental measure to reflect operating performance. SmartStop defines FFO consistent with the standards established by the white paper on FFO approved by the board of governors of NAREIT, or the White Paper. The White Paper defines FFO as net income (loss) computed in accordance with GAAP, excluding gains or losses from sales of property and real estate related asset impairment write downs, plus depreciation and amortization and after adjustments for unconsolidated partnerships and joint ventures. Additionally, gains and losses from change in control are excluded from the determination of FFO. Adjustments for unconsolidated partnerships and joint ventures are calculated to reflect FFO on the same basis. SmartStop’s FFO calculation complies with NAREIT’s policy described above.

FFO, as Adjusted

SmartStop uses FFO, as adjusted, as an additional non-GAAP financial measure to evaluate their operating performance. FFO, as adjusted, provides investors with supplemental performance information that is consistent with the performance models and analysis used by management. In addition, FFO, as adjusted, is a measure used among SmartStop’s peer group, which includes publicly traded REITs. Further, SmartStop believes FFO, as adjusted, is useful in comparing the sustainability of their operating performance with the sustainability of the operating performance of other real estate companies.

In determining FFO, as adjusted, SmartStop makes further adjustments to the NAREIT computation of FFO to exclude the effects of non-real estate related asset impairments and intangible amortization, acquisition related costs, other write-offs incurred in connection with acquisitions, contingent earnout expenses, accretion of fair value of debt adjustments, amortization of debt issuance costs, gains or losses from extinguishment of debt, adjustments of deferred tax assets and liabilities, realized and unrealized gains/losses on foreign exchange transactions, gains/losses on certain foreign exchange and interest rate derivatives not designated for hedge accounting, provision for (recovery of) non-cash reserves, and other select non-recurring income or expense items which SmartStop believes are not indicative of their overall long-term operating performance. SmartStop excludes these items from GAAP net income (loss) to arrive at FFO, as adjusted, as they are not the primary drivers in their decision-making process and excluding these items provides investors a view of their continuing operating portfolio performance over time, which in any respective period may experience fluctuations in such acquisition, merger or other similar activities that are not of a long-term operating performance nature. FFO, as adjusted, also reflects adjustments for unconsolidated partnerships and jointly owned investments. SmartStop uses FFO, as adjusted, as one measure of their operating performance when they formulate corporate goals and evaluate the effectiveness of their strategies.

Presentation of FFO and FFO, as adjusted, is intended to provide useful information to investors as they compare the operating performance of different REITs. However, not all REITs calculate FFO and FFO, as adjusted, the same way, so comparisons with other REITs may not be meaningful. Furthermore, FFO and FFO, as adjusted, are not necessarily indicative of cash flow available to fund cash needs and should not be considered as an alternative to net income (loss) as an indication of SmartStop’s performance, as an alternative to cash flows from operations as an indication of SmartStop’s liquidity or indicative of funds available to fund their cash needs including their ability to make distributions to their stockholders. FFO and FFO, as adjusted, should be reviewed in conjunction with other measurements as an indication of SmartStop’s performance.

Neither the SEC, NAREIT, nor any other regulatory body has passed judgment on the acceptability of the adjustments to FFO that SmartStop uses to calculate FFO, as adjusted. In the future, the SEC, NAREIT or another regulatory body may decide to standardize the allowable adjustments across the REIT industry and SmartStop may have to adjust its calculation and characterization of FFO, as adjusted.
