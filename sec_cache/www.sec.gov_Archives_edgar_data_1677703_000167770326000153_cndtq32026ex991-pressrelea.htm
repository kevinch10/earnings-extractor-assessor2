Conduent Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results; Reaffirms 2026 Outlook, Bolsters Liquidity Position and Completes Transit Sale

Key Q3 2026 Highlights (from Continuing Operations)
•Revenue: $534 million
•Pre-tax Income (Loss): $(35) million
•Adj. EBITDA(1) : $36 million, up 16.1% year over year
•Adj. EBITDA Margin(1): 6.7%, up 160 basis points year over year
•New Business Signings ACV(2): $61M
•Cost transformation: Identified $106M of $120M of cost-savings target, contributing to significant EBITDA margin improvement
•Financial position: Completed financing of $447 million in revolving credit commitments, maturing in May 2029
•FY 2026 outlook: Reaffirmed revenue of $2.15 billion–$2.25 billion and Adjusted EBITDA of $140 million–$170 million

FLORHAM PARK, NJ, November 5, 2026 - Conduent Incorporated (Nasdaq: CNDT), a global technology-enabled operating partner, today announced its third quarter 2026 financial results, highlighting continued execution of ASCEND 2026–2028. The company’s transformation plan is focused on standardizing operations and infrastructure, specializing in core solutions where it can lead and win, and scaling go-to-market capabilities around priority industries and opportunities.

Harsha V. Agadi, Chief Executive Officer, stated: “In the third quarter, we continued to make progress in improving Conduent’s operating performance. Adjusted EBITDA increased year-over-year and sequentially, and we are reaffirming our full-year 2026 outlook."

“We have taken decisive steps to strengthen our financial foundation, including refinancing our existing $357 million revolving credit facilities and adding $90 million of new commitments, while simplifying our portfolio and reducing costs. To date, we have identified $106 million in cost savings toward our $120 million target. At the same time, we continue to build our Commercial and Government businesses, with $249 million in year-to-date new-business ACV wins and $473 million in TCV wins."

Performance Commentary
Q3 2026 income (loss) before taxes from continuing operations was $(35) million, compared with $(38) million in the prior year period, primarily reflecting cost efficiencies, which more than offset lower revenue and higher restructuring and interest costs.

Q3 2026 Adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations was $36 million, compared with $31 million in the prior-year period, an increase of 16.1%. Adjusted EBITDA margin increased to 6.7%, compared with 5.1% in Q3 2025 and 3.0% in Q2 2026, reflecting lower expenses and cost optimization initiatives.

Q3 2026 cash flow from operating activities was $(107) million, compared with $(39) million in the prior-year period, primarily reflecting working-capital impacts, due to a temporary pause of the company's receivables factoring facility. Year-to-date cash flow from operating activities was $(108) million, compared with $(112) million in the prior-year period.

Free Cash Flow

Free Cash Flow is defined as cash flows from operating activities as reported on the consolidated statement of cash flows, less cost of additions to land, buildings and equipment, cost of additions to internal use software, and proceeds from sales of land, buildings and equipment, as applicable. We use the non-GAAP measure of Free Cash Flow as a criterion of liquidity. We use Free Cash Flow as a measure of liquidity to determine amounts we can reinvest in our core businesses, such as amounts available to make acquisitions and invest in land, buildings and equipment and internal use software, after required payments on debt. In order to provide a meaningful basis for comparison, we are providing information with respect to our Free Cash Flow reconciled to cash flow provided by operating activities, which we believe to be the most directly comparable measure under U.S. GAAP.

Adjusted Free Cash Flow

Adjusted Free Cash Flow is defined as Free Cash Flow from above plus adjustments for transaction costs, proceeds from failed sale-leaseback transactions and certain other identified adjustments, as applicable. We use Adjusted Free Cash Flow, in addition to Free Cash Flow, to provide supplemental information to our investors concerning our ability to generate cash from our ongoing operating activities; by excluding these items, we believe we provide useful additional information to our investors to help them further understand our ability to generate cash period-over-period as well as added information on comparability to our competitors. Such as with Free Cash Flow information, as so adjusted, it is specifically not intended to provide amounts available for discretionary spending. We have added certain adjustments to account for items which we do not believe reflect our core business or operating performance, and we computed all periods with such adjusted costs.

Revenue at Constant Currency

To better understand trends in our business, we believe that it is helpful to adjust revenue to exclude the impact of changes in the translation of foreign currencies into U.S. Dollars. We refer to this Adjusted Revenue as “constant currency.” Currency impact is determined as the difference between actual growth rates and constant currency growth rates. This currency impact is calculated by translating the current period activity in local currency using the comparable prior-year period's currency translation rate.
