Tarsus Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Generated record XDEMVY® net product sales of $194.6 million in the third quarter, an increase of approximately 64% year-over-year

Surpassed one million XDEMVY bottles dispensed and $1 billion in cumulative net product sales since launch

Appointed Neera Clase as Chief Commercial Officer

Management to host conference call today, November 3, 2026, at 1:30 p.m. PT / 4:30 p.m. ET

IRVINE, Calif., November 3, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Tarsus Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (NASDAQ: TARS) today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Ten years ago, we started Tarsus with the conviction that patients with overlooked diseases deserved better,” said Bobby Azamian, M.D., Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer and Chairman of Tarsus. “XDEMVY has shown what that focus can achieve. In just three years since launch, we have dispensed more than one million bottles and generated $1 billion in cumulative net product sales, and with continued robust growth, we are only getting started. That strength is helping us build one of the most differentiated pipelines in eye care, with the potential to bring five medicines to patients in the next five years. As we become the next leader in eye care, what drives us is the patient still searching for a better treatment and the possibility that our next medicine can provide it.”

Recent Business and Clinical Highlights
•XDEMVY generated third quarter 2026 net product sales of $194.6 million, a year-over-year increase of approximately 64%.
◦Since launch, more than one million bottles of XDEMVY have been dispensed and cumulative net product sales have surpassed $1 billion, as the first and only FDA-approved treatment for Demodex blepharitis (DB) reaches more patients.
◦The average number of Eye Care Professionals (ECPs) prescribing XDEMVY at least daily increased approximately 20% from the beginning to the end of the third quarter, amidst a seasonally softer quarter in prescription eye care.
◦Direct-to-consumer initiatives drove a 75% increase in high-value actions and we saw a higher prescription conversion than last year.
◦Retreatment continued to become a more established part of care, with refills accounting for approximately 20% of XDEMVY prescriptions in the third quarter.

•Presented 11 scientific abstracts at the American Academy of Optometry, adding to the real-world evidence for XDEMVY and understanding of Demodex blepharitis. Key findings included:
◦Retreatment experience: Among ECPs with retreatment experience, 98% were satisfied with XDEMVY’s efficacy and tolerability.
◦Disease recognition: DB was found in 65% of patients presenting with chalazia or hordeola, rising to 91% among those with recurrent cases, reinforcing the importance of checking for DB in routine eye care.


◦Longer-term outcomes: In an ongoing real-world observational study, 73% of XDEMVY-treated patients reported improvement in itchy eyelids, a key symptom of DB, at nine months, compared with 29% of patients receiving non-lotilaner options.

•Tarsus continues to advance one of the most compelling pipelines in eye care, with multiple upcoming clinical milestones.
◦TP-04: The Phase 2 Kore trial for the potential treatment of ocular rosacea continues to progress, with topline data expected in the first half of 2027.
◦TP-05: Completed enrollment in the Phase 2 Calliope trial for the potential prevention of Lyme disease. Topline results are expected in the first half of 2027 and are anticipated to support a Phase 3-ready package.
◦IRX-101: Plan to initiate the Phase 3 Comfort study evaluating an investigational ocular antiseptic for patients receiving intravitreal injections in the first half of 2027, with topline results expected in 2028.
◦Gildeuretinol: Enrollment continues to progress in the global Phase 3 Northstar trial for the potential treatment of Stargardt disease, with topline results expected in 2029.

•Completed the acquisitions of iRenix Medical (IRX-101) and Alkeus Pharmaceuticals (Gildeuretinol).
◦IRX-101 is an investigational ocular antiseptic with the potential to improve the standard of care by reducing post-procedural pain and corneal toxicity in patients receiving intravitreal injections.
◦Gildeuretinol is an investigational once-daily oral medicine designed to target the underlying biology of Stargardt disease, a potentially blinding inherited retinal disease with no FDA-approved treatment.

•Appointed Neera Clase as Chief Commercial Officer
◦Ms. Clase has been instrumental in creating a new market for XDEMVY, building our commercial team, and securing broad coverage.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•    Product sales, net: were $194.6 million compared to $118.7 million for the same period in 2025, driven by higher volume and improvements in the gross-to-net discounts.

•    Cost of sales: were $13.9 million compared to $8.3 million for the same period in 2025, due to manufacturing costs related to XDEMVY, the royalty Tarsus pays on net product sales, and amortization expense for the milestone payments made to the Company’s licensor, which are being amortized over its remaining useful life. Gross margins remained consistent at approximately 93% for both periods.

•    Research and development (R&D) expenses: were $44.2 million compared to $16.3 million for the same period in 2025. Of this $27.9 million increase, $13.7 million was attributable to the acquisition of Alkeus, consisting of $7.1 million of payroll and personnel-related costs (including $5.8 million of non-cash stock-based compensation), $3.1 million of gildeuretinol program expenses, $3.0 million of acquired inventory, and $0.4 million of transaction-related costs.

The remaining $14.2 million R&D increase was primarily due to $6.8 million of TP-05 program expenses related to the Company’s Calliope trial, $3.8 million of payroll and personnel-related costs (including non-cash stock-based compensation), $1.8 million of TP-04 program expenses, $0.8 million of IRX-101 program expenses, and $2.0 million of other early-stage program expenses; the increases were partially offset by a $1.1 million decrease in TP-03 program expenses. Total R&D non-cash stock-based compensation expense incurred was $10.2 million, compared with $3.0 million in the same period in 2025.

•    Acquired in-process research and development (IPR&D) expenses: were $632.2 million for the period, consisting of $81.0 million related to the acquisition of iRenix and $551.2 million related to the acquisition of Alkeus, representing the acquisition-date fair value of the IRX-101 and gildeuretinol IPR&D that were


expensed because they have no alternative future use. There was no acquired IPR&D expense for the same period in 2025.

•    Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses: were $168.0 million compared to $108.6 million for the same period in 2025. Of this $59.4 million increase, $29.8 million was attributable to the acquisition of Alkeus, consisting of $6.1 million of payroll and personnel-related costs (including $5.7 million of non-cash stock-based compensation), $23.5 million of transaction-related and integration costs, and $0.2 million of professional services.

The remaining $29.6 million SG&A increase was due primarily to $11.7 million of commercial and marketing costs, including DTC advertising costs, as the Company continued to expand promotional efforts for XDEMVY, $9.6 million of costs associated with patient support functions, information technology, legal, and professional services, and $8.3 million of payroll and personnel-related costs for commercial and corporate employee additions to support the Company’s continued growth and expansion of its commercial leadership team. Total SG&A non-cash stock-based compensation expense was $16.8 million, compared with $9.1 million in the same period in 2025.

•Change in Fair Value of Contingent Consideration: The $1.6 million decrease in fair value of the contingent consideration related to the Alkeus regulatory milestone was primarily driven by a one percentage point increase in the discount rate between the closing date and period end.

•    Net loss: was $668.3 million, compared to $12.6 million for the same period in 2025. Basic and diluted net loss per share was $(14.40), compared with $(0.30) for the same period in 2025.

•    Cash position: As of September 30, 2026, cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were $309.6 million.

About XDEMVY®
XDEMVY (lotilaner ophthalmic solution) 0.25%, formerly known as TP-03, is a novel prescription eye drop designed to treat Demodex blepharitis by targeting and eradicating the root cause of the disease – Demodex mite infestation. XDEMVY was evaluated in two pivotal trials involving over 800 patients with twice-daily dosing for six weeks. Both trials met the primary endpoint and all secondary endpoints, with statistical significance and no serious treatment-related adverse events. Most patients found the XDEMVY eye drop to be neutral to very comfortable. The most common ocular adverse reactions observed in the studies were instillation site stinging and burning which was reported in 10% of patients. Other ocular adverse reactions reported by less than 2% of patients were chalazion/hordeolum (stye) and punctate keratitis.

XDEMVY Indication and Important Safety Information

INDICATIONS AND USAGE
XDEMVY is indicated for the treatment of Demodex blepharitis.



Most common side effects: The most common side effect in clinical trials was stinging and burning in 10% of patients. Other side effects in less than 2% of patients were chalazion/hordeolum and punctate keratitis.

For additional information, please see full prescribing information available at https://xdemvy.com/.

About TP-03
TP-03 (lotilaner ophthalmic solution) 0.25% is a novel therapeutic designed to treat Demodex blepharitis by targeting and eradicating the root cause of disease – Demodex mite infestation. It was approved by the FDA in 2023 under the brand name XDEMVY® for the treatment of Demodex blepharitis. Lotilaner is a well-characterized anti-parasitic agent that paralyzes and eradicates Demodex mites by selectively inhibiting parasite-specific gamma-aminobutyric acid-gated chloride (GABA-Cl) channels. It is a highly lipophilic molecule, which may promote its uptake in the oily sebum of the eyelash follicles where the mites reside.

About TP-04
TP-04 is an investigational sterile aqueous gel formulation of lotilaner. Tarsus is studying TP-04 for the potential treatment of ocular rosacea (OR).

About TP-05
TP-05 is an investigational oral systemic formulation of lotilaner. TP-05 is believed to be the only non-vaccine, drug-based, preventative therapeutic in development designed to kill ticks to potentially prevent Lyme disease transmission.

About IRX-101
IRX-101 is an investigational ocular antiseptic based on a stable aqueous chlorine dioxide solution that is being developed for the potential to reduce post-procedural pain and corneal toxicity in patients receiving intravitreal therapy.

About gildeuretinol
Gildeuretinol is an investigational small molecule with once-daily oral formulation targeting toxic Vitamin A dimerization in the retina with a promising profile across 400+ individuals studied, and generally well tolerated.

About Tarsus Pharmaceuticals, Inc.
Tarsus Pharmaceuticals, Inc. applies proven science and new technology to revolutionize treatment for patients, starting with eye care. Tarsus is advancing its pipeline to address several diseases with high unmet need across a range of therapeutic categories, including eye care, dermatology, and infectious disease prevention.
