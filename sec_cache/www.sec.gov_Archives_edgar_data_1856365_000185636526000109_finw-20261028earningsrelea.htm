FINWISE BANCORP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
- Loan Originations of $2.1 Billion -
- Net Income of $10.0 Million -
- Diluted Earnings Per Share of $0.73 -

MURRAY, UTAH — October 28, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — FinWise Bancorp (NASDAQ: FINW) (“FinWise”, the “Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), parent company of FinWise Bank (the “Bank”), today announced results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Loan originations totaled $2.1 billion, compared to $1.6 billion for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and $1.8 billion for the third quarter of the prior year
•Period end credit enhanced loan balances outstanding reached $146.2 million in the third quarter, compared to $74.2 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and $41.4 million for the third quarter of the prior year
•Net interest income was $41.9 million, compared to $28.4 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and $18.6 million for the third quarter of the prior year
•Net income was $10.0 million, compared to $2.1 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and $4.9 million for the third quarter of the prior year
•Net income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 includes a $6.5 million bargain purchase gain resulting from the July 20, 2026 acquisition of certain assets and assumption of certain liabilities of Tallied Technologies Inc. and a $0.4 million loss from discontinued operations
•Diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) were $0.73 for the quarter, compared to $0.15 for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and $0.34 for the third quarter of the prior year
•Efficiency ratio1 was 41.6%, compared to 54.9% for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and 47.6% for the third quarter of the prior year
•Nonperforming loan balances were $37.1 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $37.7 million as of June 30, 2026, and $43.1 million as of September 30, 2025. Nonperforming loan balances guaranteed by the Small Business Administration (“SBA”) were $17.7 million, $19.1 million, and $23.6 million as of September 30, 2026, June 30, 2026, and September 30, 2025, respectively

“This quarter demonstrates the tangible progress we have made in broadening our platform and building a more capital-efficient sponsor bank,” said Jim Noone, CEO of FinWise Bancorp. “We originated $2.1 billion in loans and we nearly doubled our credit-enhanced balances to $146 million in the quarter. Even after the cost of those partner arrangements, net interest income rose 17%1 from the second quarter. We also recognized a one-time gain from our first acquisition transaction.

“Credit performance improved during the quarter. Net charge-offs on loans without credit enhancement were $2.6 million in the quarter, well below the range of the last few quarters. Non-performing asset balances also decreased quarter-over-quarter. With tangible book value up 9% over the past year, a leverage ratio of 18.1%, a strong pipeline of prospective partners with the potential to contribute more meaningfully to future revenue, and continued share repurchases, we have the capital strength, teams and operating environment to execute. We remain committed to disciplined execution, ongoing credit quality improvement, and delivering long-term value for our shareholders."

Credit Card Business: Strategic Repositioning and Discontinued Operations
In July 2026, FinWise completed its acquisition of certain assets and assumption of certain liabilities of Tallied Technologies, Inc. (“Tallied”). The transaction added card issuing and program management technology, along with an experienced team, that expands the Company’s ability to support credit card programs for its fintech partners. Concurrent with the acquisition, the Company determined that investing in credit card receivables was not a strategy the Company would pursue, and the Board of Directors approved a plan to exit the credit card portfolio business and to divest the credit card loan portfolio and certain acquired assets and liabilities while retaining the technology and credit card servicing platform. The disposal group consists of the credit card portfolio as well as the program sponsor relationship, program records, and associated cardholder rewards liability acquired as part of the Tallied transaction. Accordingly, the results of the credit card business are presented as discontinued operations for all periods presented, and the related assets and liabilities are classified as assets and liabilities of discontinued operations on the balance sheet as of September 30, 2026. The Company is retaining the technology platform and development team to help complete the platform build out to be used to support our

strategy to service credit card programs and the payments needs of our fintech partners as part of our banking-as-a service strategy.

For the third quarter of 2026, net income from continuing operations was $10.4 million, or $0.76 per diluted share, including a bargain purchase gain of $6.5 million. Net loss from discontinued operations was $0.4 million. The Company expects to complete the sale of its credit card business, including certain credit card assets acquired in the Tallied transaction, by the second quarter of 2027.

Loan Originations
Loan originations totaled $2.1 billion for the third quarter of 2026, an increase from the $1.6 billion recorded in the prior quarter and an increase from the $1.8 billion recorded in the prior year period. The growth in originations compared to both the prior quarter and the prior year period primarily reflects the seasonality of originations by student loan lenders, the rapid growth of a credit enhancement lending relationship, and an increase in volume from certain established strategic programs.

Net Interest Income and Net Interest Margin
Net interest income was $41.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $28.4 million for the prior quarter and $18.6 million for the prior year period. The increase from the prior quarter was primarily due to growth in the credit-enhanced loan portfolio and a decrease in the migration of performing loans to nonperforming loans, which resulted in a lower reversal of interest on nonaccrual loans and contributed to an increase in the average yield on loans held-for-investment. The increase from the prior year period was primarily due to the same growth in credit enhanced loans, as well as a change in estimate, based on additional information and experience, regarding the allocation of interest received on credit enhanced loans in excess of the amount FinWise retains. FinWise now estimates that all excess interest is attributable to servicing and credit guarantee expense. In the prior year, it had estimated that a portion was attributable to origination costs, or finders’ fees, which was reported in net interest income. Because this change in estimate was reflected in both the current and prior quarters, it did not affect the comparison to the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

Net interest margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 19.58%, compared to 13.83% for the prior quarter and 9.09% for the prior year period. The increase in net interest margin from the prior quarter was primarily due to growth in the higher-yielding credit-enhanced loan portfolio and a decrease in the migration of performing loans to nonaccrual status, which resulted in lower reversals of interest on nonaccrual loans, partially offset by a higher average rate paid on time certificates of deposit. The increase in net interest margin from the prior-year period was primarily due to growth in the higher-yielding credit-enhanced loan portfolio and higher yields on loans held-for-investment, the change in estimated allocation of excess interest as previously described, and lower rates paid on deposits.

Tax Rate
The Company’s effective tax rate was 21.0% for the third quarter of 2026, down from 23.9% for the prior quarter and 23.7% for the prior year period. The lower effective tax rate in the third quarter is primarily driven by the $6.5 million bargain purchase gain recognized in connection with the Tallied acquisition, as it is excluded from the calculation of income tax because the related deferred taxes were recorded in acquisition accounting. Partially offsetting this benefit were $0.6 million of discrete tax expense related to state income tax refunds received during the quarter, as well as a higher estimated annual effective tax rate attributable to nondeductible compensation.

Net Income
Net income was $10.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $2.1 million for the prior quarter and $4.9 million for the prior year period. Net income from continuing operations was $10.4 million for the third quarter, compared to $1.8 million for the prior quarter and $4.9 million for the prior year period. Third quarter results included a non-recurring, pre-tax bargain purchase gain of $6.5 million recognized in connection with the Tallied acquisition; this gain was exempt from income tax as the related deferred taxes were recorded in acquisition accounting. Discontinued operations resulted in a loss, net of tax, of $0.4 million for the third quarter, compared to income, net of tax, of $0.4 million for the prior quarter. The remaining changes in net income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 relative to both the prior quarter and prior year period, are otherwise generally attributable to the factors discussed in the foregoing sections.

Asset Quality
The recorded balances of nonperforming loans were $37.1 million, or 6.3% of total loans held-for-investment, as of September 30, 2026, compared to $37.7 million, or 7.2% of total loans held-for-investment, as of June 30, 2026 and $43.1 million, or 7.7% of total loans held-for-investment, as of September 30, 2025. The balances of nonperforming loans guaranteed by the SBA were $17.7 million, $19.1 million, and $23.6 million as of September 30, 2026, June 30, 2026, and September 30, 2025, respectively. The decrease in nonperforming loans from the prior quarter and prior year period was primarily attributable to paydowns resulting from the sale of real property collateralizing nonperforming SBA 7(a) and commercial real estate loans. The decrease in nonperforming loans as a percentage of total loans held-for-investment from the prior quarter also reflects growth in credit-enhanced loans held-for-investment. The Company’s allowance for credit losses to total loans held-for-investment was 13.6% as of September 30, 2026 compared to 8.3% as of June 30, 2026 and 4.6% as of September 30, 2025. The increase in the ratio from the prior quarter and prior year period was primarily due to the provision for credit losses related to the growth of the credit enhanced loan balances.

The Company’s net charge-offs were $10.6 million, $11.4 million, and $3.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, June 30, 2026, and September 30, 2025, respectively. The decrease in net charge-offs from the prior quarter resulted primarily from lower charge-offs in the traditional bank portfolio, partially offset by higher charge-offs associated with credit-enhanced Strategic Program loans. The increase from the third quarter of 2025 resulted primarily from higher charge-offs associated with credit-enhanced Strategic Program loans as that program increased in size and matured. Charge-offs for the traditional bank portfolio totaled $1.1 million in the third quarter compared to $3.3 million in the prior quarter and $0.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease from the prior quarter reflects lower charge-offs on owner-occupied commercial real estate and commercial and industrial loans, while the increase from the prior year period primarily reflects charge-offs on owner-occupied commercial real estate loans resulting from the resolution of specific nonperforming loans.

FinWise has entered into agreements with certain of its Strategic Program service providers pursuant to which they provide credit enhancement on loans which protects the Bank by indemnifying or reimbursing the Bank for incurred credit and fraud losses. We estimate and record a provision for expected losses for these Strategic Program loans in accordance with GAAP, which requires estimation of the provision without consideration of the credit enhancement. When the provision for expected losses over the life of the loans that are subject to such credit enhancement is recorded, a credit enhancement asset reflecting the future recovery of those estimated credit losses pursuant to the strategic partner’s guarantee to assume the Bank’s credit losses on each of the loans in the respective guaranteed portfolio is also recorded on the balance sheet in the form of non-interest income (credit enhancement income). Reimbursement or indemnification for incurred losses is provided for in the form of a deposit reserve account that is replenished periodically by the respective Strategic Program service provider. The credit enhancement asset is reduced as credit enhancement payments and recoveries are received from the Strategic Program service provider or taken from its cash reserve account. If the Strategic Program service provider is unable to fulfill its contracted obligations under its credit enhancement agreement, then the Bank could be exposed to the loss of the reimbursement and credit enhancement income as a result of this counterparty risk. In the event the Strategic Program service provider is not able to perform according to the contractual terms, the Bank is entitled to receive all the income on the loans. The Bank incurs expenses for the amounts owed to the strategic partner for the credit guarantee and for servicing of the credit enhanced portfolio, if applicable (credit enhancement program expenses).
