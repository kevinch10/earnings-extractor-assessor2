Target Corporation Reports Third Quarter Earnings

•Third quarter net sales grew 7.1 percent over last year, with comparable sales growth of 5.6 percent driven by a 5.0 percent increase in comparable traffic.
◦Store comparable sales grew 4.6 percent, and digital comparable sales grew 9.8 percent.
◦Net sales grew across a majority of core merchandising categories versus a year ago, including nearly 27 percent growth in Fun 101, more than 9 percent growth in Beauty, and nearly 7 percent growth in Food and Beverage.
◦Net sales grew in all three months of the quarter, with the strongest performance during the Back-to-School and -College seasons.
◦On a two-year basis, net sales and traffic growth accelerated relative to the first half of the year.
◦Non-merchandise sales grew nearly 20 percent, reflecting strong growth in Roundel ad revenue, Target Circle Card profit sharing revenue, Target Circle 360 membership revenue, and the Target+ marketplace.
•Third quarter GAAP and Adjusted EPS1 were $2.65 compared with last year’s GAAP EPS of $1.51 and Adjusted EPS of $1.78.
◦Third quarter 2026 results included tariff refund2 benefits of $0.50. Excluding tariff refunds, GAAP EPS would have increased 42 percent, and Adjusted EPS would have increased 21 percent, when compared with the prior year.
•This holiday season, Target will deliver style, design and newness at an incredible value, with more than 8,000 new gifts — over half exclusive to Target — including thousands priced at $5 and under, alongside new AI-powered shopping experiences, a $30 Thanksgiving meal for six3, and early Black Friday savings and daily deals through December 24th.
◦Month-to-date, November net sales are in line with the Company's guidance and expectations, accelerating from October sales trends.

MINNEAPOLIS (November 18, 2026) – Target Corporation (NYSE: TGT) today announced its third quarter 2026 financial results.

The Company reported third quarter GAAP and Adjusted earnings per share (EPS) of $2.65, compared with prior-year GAAP EPS of $1.51 and Adjusted EPS of $1.78. Third quarter 2026 results include $301 million of pretax tariff refund benefits within gross margin and operating income. These gains contributed $228 million to net earnings and $0.50 to both GAAP and Adjusted EPS. The attached tables provide reconciliations of non-GAAP to GAAP measures. All earnings per share figures are calculated on a diluted basis.

“Our third-quarter results reflect continued progress against our strategic priorities, centered on serving busy families with our signature blend of style, design and value,” said Michael Fiddelke, Chief Executive Officer of Target. “Our investments are building on one another—creating momentum in our business and further defining our unique lane in retail. As we head into the all-important holiday season, we’re focused on offering the best of Target to our guests—from finding just the right gifts for all the people they love to bringing their holiday traditions and celebrations to life—with value they can feel great about.”

Guidance4

With continued strong performance through the third quarter, the Company has the following updated expectations for 2026:
•Full-year net sales growth in a range around 6 percent, one percentage point higher than the prior guidance range.
•Full-year 2026 operating income margin rate in a range around 6.2 percent, including approximately 1.1 percentage points of benefit from tariff refunds recognized to date.
•An updated GAAP and Adjusted EPS guidance range of $10.65 to $11.40, which includes approximately $2.14 in tariff refund benefits recognized to date.

Additionally, building on the strong returns generated by capital investments in 2026, the Company expects to invest between $5 billion and $6 billion in capital expenditures in 2027.

Operating Results
Net Sales of $27.1 billion in the third quarter were 7.1 percent higher than last year, reflecting a 6.9 percent increase in merchandise sales and a 19.3 percent increase in non-merchandise sales. Comparable sales grew 5.6 percent in the third quarter, reflecting a comparable store sales increase of 4.6 percent and comparable digital sales increase of 9.8 percent. Third quarter operating income, which included a $301 million benefit from tariff refunds, was $1.7 billion, compared with prior-year GAAP operating income of $0.9 billion and Adjusted operating income of $1.1 billion. Operating income margin rate of 6.2 percent, which included 1.1 percentage points of benefit from the tariff refunds, increased meaningfully from the prior-year GAAP operating income margin rate of 3.8 percent and Adjusted operating income rate of 4.4 percent. Third quarter gross margin rate was 30.5 percent, expanding 2.3 percentage points and reflecting 1.1 percentage points of benefit from tariff refunds. Excluding tariff refunds, third quarter gross margin rate would have expanded approximately 1.2 percentage points over the prior year rate of 28.2 percent, reflecting the comparison against last year’s elevated markdowns and tariff rates, as well as growth in non-merchandise sales and the benefit of productivity improvements at supply chain facilities. These benefits were partially offset by elevated freight and fuel-related costs, continued investments in value, and modest pressure from category sales mix. Third quarter GAAP and Adjusted SG&A expense rate was 21.9 percent, compared with prior-year GAAP SG&A expense rate of 21.9 percent and Adjusted SG&A expense rate of 21.3 percent, which excluded non-recurring costs related to our business transformation initiative. This increase in Adjusted SG&A rate reflects the impact of higher incentive compensation, additional store labor hours, as well as planned investments in new and existing stores, partially offset by the leverage benefit of strong topline growth.

Interest Expense and Taxes
The Company’s third quarter 2026 net interest expense was $95 million, compared with $115 million last year, reflecting higher interest income in the current year.

Third quarter 2026 effective income tax rate increased to 23.9 percent, compared with the prior year rate of 19.8 percent, primarily driven by higher tax benefits recognized in the prior year as well as higher pretax earnings in the current year.

Capital Deployment and Return on Invested Capital
Third quarter capital expenditures of $1.4 billion were $0.4 billion higher than last year, driven primarily by increased investments in new and existing stores.

The Company paid dividends of $527 million in the third quarter, compared with $518 million last year, reflecting a 1.8 percent increase in the dividend per share.

The Company repurchased $251 million of its shares in the third quarter, retiring 1.6 million shares of common stock at an average price of $155.49. As of the end of the quarter, the Company had approximately $8.0 billion of remaining capacity under the repurchase program approved by Target’s Board of Directors in August 2021.

For the trailing twelve months through third quarter 2026, after-tax return on invested capital (ROIC) was 16.5 percent, compared with 13.4 percent for the trailing twelve months through third quarter 2025.
