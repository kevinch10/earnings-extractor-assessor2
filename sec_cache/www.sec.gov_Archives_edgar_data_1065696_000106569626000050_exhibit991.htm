LKQ CORPORATION ANNOUNCES RESULTS FOR THIRD QUARTER 2026

Antioch, TENN (October 29, 2026) -- LKQ Corporation (Nasdaq: LKQ) today reported third quarter 2026 financial results.

Commenting on the third quarter, John Mendel, Chairman of the Board of LKQ, said: “LKQ remains a fundamentally strong enterprise, supported by its scale, leading positions across key markets, and global distribution platform. At the same time, our recent financial and operating performance has not reflected the strength of the business or met the standards we expect, particularly in Europe. The Board has been deeply engaged in addressing these challenges, and the appointment of a new CEO, leadership changes in Europe, and the engagement of Alvarez & Marsal are important steps in a broader effort to improve execution and financial performance. We believe LKQ has significant strengths that are not currently reflected in its operating performance, and our focus is on translating those strengths into improved performance and increased value for our shareholders.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results

Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $3.3 billion, a decrease of 5.1% compared to $3.5 billion for the third quarter of 2025. Total parts and services revenue decreased 5.6%, which included a 5.4% decrease in parts and services organic revenue, a 0.7% decrease from foreign exchange rates year over year, and the net impact of acquisitions and divestitures, which increased revenue by 0.6%.

Net loss2 was $24 million, including $111 million of pre-tax non-cash impairment charges, compared to net income of $178 million for the same period of 2025. Diluted loss per share2 was $(0.09) compared to diluted earnings per share2 of $0.69 for the same period of 2025.

On an adjusted basis, net income1,2 was $127 million compared to $216 million for the same period of 2025. Adjusted diluted earnings per share1,2 was $0.50 compared to $0.84 for the same period of 2025.

“The third quarter was characterized by weakness in our Europe segment, driven by operational issues related to the ERP implementation in Germany and volume declines in the United Kingdom and Benelux. Specialty was modestly below plan. North America performed in line with expectations despite a softer demand environment,” said Rick Galloway, Chief Financial Officer of LKQ.

Cash Flow and Balance Sheet

Cash flow from operations3 and free cash flow1,3 were $411 million and $372 million, respectively, for the third quarter of 2026. Cash flow from operations3 and free cash flow1,3 were $466 million and $336 million, respectively, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. As of September 30, 2026, the balance sheet reflected total debt of $3.7 billion and total leverage, as defined in our credit facility, was 2.8x EBITDA.

Returning Capital to Shareholders

During the third quarter of 2026, the Company invested $30 million to repurchase 1.2 million shares of its common stock and distributed $76 million in cash dividends. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company returned $313 million to its shareholders by investing $83 million to repurchase 3.1 million shares of its common stock and distributing $230 million in cash dividends. Since initiating the stock repurchase program in late October 2018, the Company has repurchased approximately 72 million shares of its common stock for a total of $3.0 billion through September 30, 2026. As of September 30, 2026, there was $1.5 billion remaining on the authorization.

Strategic Review

Alongside the recent leadership changes, the Board has decided to suspend the strategic review, including the Specialty segment sales process. This will provide the new leadership team the opportunity to conduct a comprehensive assessment of the business and evaluate the full range of value‑creation pathways available to the Company.

The Board will continue to oversee the strategic direction, protect shareholder interests, and ensure accountability. This includes evaluating opportunities that it believes could enhance shareholder value and optimize the Company’s strategic positioning over the long term.

Other Events

On October 23, 2026, the Company announced that the Board appointed Salvatore A. “Sal” Abbate as President and Chief Executive Officer, effective December 15, 2026. Mr. Abbate brings more than three decades of experience leading large, complex distribution and industrial businesses. As CEO of Veritiv Corporation, he led a significant transformation of the company, with its share price increasing more than 13-fold during his tenure, culminating in its $2.6 billion sale to Clayton, Dubilier & Rice in 2023. Before becoming CEO, he served as Veritiv’s Chief Operating Officer and Chief Commercial Officer. Earlier in his career, Mr. Abbate held senior leadership roles at Andersen Windows & Doors, Solutia, Inc., now part of Eastman Chemical Company, and Armstrong World Industries, with responsibilities spanning sales, marketing, distribution, logistics, manufacturing, strategy and process improvement.

On October 23, 2026 the Company announced that it is withdrawing its full-year 2026 guidance, which was most recently updated on July 30, 2026.

About LKQ Corporation

LKQ Corporation (www.lkqcorp.com) is a leading provider of alternative and specialty parts to repair and accessorize automobiles and other vehicles. LKQ has operations in North America, Europe and Taiwan. LKQ offers its customers a broad range of OEM recycled and aftermarket parts, replacement systems, components, equipment, and services to repair and accessorize automobiles, trucks, and recreational and performance vehicles.

We have presented Segment EBITDA solely as a supplemental disclosure that offers investors, securities analysts and other interested parties useful information to evaluate our segment profit and loss and underlying trends in our ongoing operations. We calculate Segment EBITDA as Net Income and Loss excluding net income and loss attributable to noncontrolling interest; income and loss from discontinued operations; depreciation; amortization; interest; gains and losses on debt extinguishment; income tax expense; restructuring and transaction related expenses; change in fair value of contingent consideration liabilities; other gains and losses related to acquisitions, equity method investments, or divestitures; equity in losses and earnings of unconsolidated subsidiaries; equity investment fair value adjustments; impairment charges; and direct impacts of the Ukraine/Russia conflict. Our chief operating decision maker ("CODM"), who is our Chief Executive Officer, uses Segment EBITDA as the key measure of our segment profit or loss. The CODM uses Segment EBITDA to compare profitability among our segments and evaluate business strategies. This financial measure is included in the metrics used to determine incentive compensation for our senior management. We also consider Segment EBITDA to be a useful financial measure in evaluating our operating performance, as it provides investors, securities analysts and other interested parties with supplemental information regarding the underlying trends in our ongoing operations. Segment EBITDA includes revenue and expenses that are controllable by the segment. Corporate general and administrative expenses are allocated to the segments based on usage, with shared expenses apportioned based on the segment's percentage of consolidated revenue. We have presented Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Diluted Earnings per Share as we believe these measures are useful for evaluating the core operating performance of our continuing business across reporting periods and in analyzing our historical operating results. We define Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Diluted Earnings per Share as Net Income and Loss and Diluted Earnings (Loss) per Share adjusted to eliminate the impact of net income and loss attributable to noncontrolling interest, income and loss from discontinued operations, restructuring and transaction related expenses, amortization expense related to all acquired intangible assets, gains and losses on debt extinguishment, changes in fair value of contingent consideration liabilities, other gains and losses related to acquisitions, equity method investments, or divestitures, impairment charges, direct impacts of the Ukraine/Russia conflict, excess tax benefits and deficiencies from stock-based payments and any tax effect of these adjustments. The tax effect of these adjustments is calculated using the effective tax rate for the applicable period or for certain discrete items the specific tax expense or benefit for the adjustment. Given the variability and volatility of the amount of related transactions in a particular period, management believes that these costs are not core operating expenses and should be adjusted in our calculation of Adjusted Net Income. Our adjustment of the amortization of all acquisition-related intangible assets does not exclude the amortization of other assets, which represents expense that is directly attributable to ongoing operations. Management believes that the adjustment relating to amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets supplements the GAAP information with a measure that can be used to assess the comparability of operating performance. The


acquired intangible assets were recorded as part of purchase accounting and contribute to revenue generation. Amortization of intangible assets that relate to past acquisitions will recur in future periods until such intangible assets have been fully amortized. Any future acquisitions may result in the amortization of additional intangible assets. These financial measures are used by management in its decision making and overall evaluation of our operating performance and are included in the metrics used to determine incentive compensation for our senior management. Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Diluted Earnings per Share should not be construed as alternatives to Net Income and Loss or Diluted Earnings (Loss) per Share as determined in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States.
