In brief, consolidated total 3Q 2026 revenues were $1,191 (12.1%) lower than in 3Q 2025, and 9M 2026 revenues were $3,602 (12.1%) lower than in 9M 2025. The lower sales in comparison with the prior year’s periods were substantially a result of a lack of any 2026 sales to UTMD’s two previously largest customers, PendoTECH, a supplier of control devices to biopharm manufacturing companies, and UTMD’s distributor of disposable blood pressure monitoring (BPM) transducer kits in China.  Those two lost customers in 2026 represented a combined $491 in 3Q 2025 sales, and a combined $2,419 in 9M 2025 sales. Partly offsetting the PendoTECH lost sales, UTMD realized $116 in 3Q 2026 sales, and $327 in 9M 2026 sales, to new biopharm customers. However, the 2026 growth in new biopharm sales has been slower than expected. Also, UTMD realized $195 in 3Q 2026 sales to a new China BPM kit distributor for the first time since losing the prior distributor a year ago. This distributor might eventually match the prior distributor’s demand.

 

There was also an accelerated decline in worldwide (WW) sales of the Filshie Clip System (Filshie) in 3Q 2026, which were $805 (32.7%) lower than in 3Q 2025. The poor 3Q Filshie sales caused 9M 2026 Filshie sales to be $1,194 (15.7%) lower than in 9M 2025. On the other hand, Femcare Ltd, UTMD’s subsidiary in the UK which developed the Filshie Clip System, acquired Orion Medical Supplies Ltd in the UK at the end of August 2026, which added $619 in 3Q 2026 sales.

 

Changes in period-to-period foreign currency exchange rates had less than a one percentage point impact on USD sales: 3Q 2026 sales were $14 lower, and 9M 2026 sales were $170 higher, than would have been the case with the same exchange rates in 2025. Further details about UTMD’s period-to-period sales comparisons are provided later in this press release, and will also be explained in UTMD’s public 3Q 2026 SEC 10-Q disclosure in November.

Revenues (sales) -3Q 2026

 

Total consolidated 3Q 2026 UTMD worldwide (WW) sales in USD terms were $8,621 compared to $9,812 in 3Q 2025.

 

Consistent with UTMD’s SEC 10-K Report at the beginning of the year, 1) sales of biopharma pressure monitoring devices and accessories to UTMD’s previously largest OEM customer, PendoTECH, were zero in 3Q 2026, which were $96 in 3Q 2025 domestic OEM sales, and 2) sales of blood pressure monitoring (BPM) kits to UTMD’s previously largest distributor outside the U.S. (OUS) located in China were also zero, which were $395 in international sales in 3Q 2025.  The combined sales to those two entities, which were zero in 3Q 2026, were $491 in 3Q 2025 and $1,082 in 3Q 2024. Offsetting the $395 lost sales to the prior China distributor were $195 3Q sales to a new China distributor.

 

Domestic sales are invoiced in USD and obviously not subject to foreign currency conversion (FX) rate fluctuations. The components of domestic sales include 1) “direct non-Filshie device sales” of UTMD’s medical devices to user facilities (and med/surg stocking distributors for hospitals), 2) “OEM sales” of components and other products manufactured by UTMD for other medical device and non-medical device companies, and 3) “domestic Filshie device sales”. UTMD separates domestic Filshie device sales from other medical device sales direct to medical facilities because UTMD is simply a distributor for Femcare in the U.S.

In the aggregate, 3Q 2026 domestic sales were 16.1% lower, at just $4,917 compared to $5,859 in 3Q 2025. Total domestic OEM sales at $677 were $41 higher than in 3Q 2025, including new domestic OEM biopharma customer sales which were $66 higher. Domestic sales of the Filshie Clip System at $656 in 3Q 2026 were $534 lower than in 3Q 2025, a weak quarter following stronger sales in first half (1H) 2026 compared to 1H 2025. Domestic direct sales of other devices in 3Q 2026 were $449 lower than in 3Q 2025, about 64% of which were due to lower neonatal device sales.

 

OUS sales in 3Q 2026 were $249 (6.3%) lower at $3,704 compared to $3,953 in 3Q 2025.  Net of the lost previous China distributor and the new distributor, BPM kit sales to China were $200 lower.  OUS Filshie device sales were $271 lower. Orion Medical contributed $619 in new sales. In 3Q 2026, 50% of OUS sales were direct to medical facilities located in Ireland, the UK, France, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, compared to 37% in 3Q 2025. The difference was due to the $619 addition of Orion Medical sales in the UK, net of OUS direct Filshie device sales which were $230 (21.8%) lower. OUS direct to end-user sales are invoiced in foreign currencies. UTMD’s Form SEC 10-Q for 3Q 2026, which will be filed in November, will provide further 3Q revenue segmentation details.

Gross Profit (GP)

 

GP results from subtracting the cost of goods sold (CGS), comprised of costs of production including direct labor, manufacturing engineering, depreciation of equipment, maintenance and repairs, quality assurance including regulatory compliance, and purchasing including freight for receiving materials from suppliers, from revenues. CGS is divided into three categories: direct labor, raw materials and manufacturing overhead (MOH).  Direct labor and raw materials are predominantly variable costs, i.e. vary directly with revenues.  MOH contains many fixed costs consistent with the Company’s infrastructure, for example, supervision, quality assurance and engineering personnel, and depreciation of fixed assets.

UTMD’s 3Q 2026 Gross Profit margin (GPM) was lower than in 3Q 2025 because WW trade shipments were lower while the Company retained the same previous labor and overhead staffing that it previously had in order to support the anticipated substantial new growth in biopharma control device manufacturing. UTMD Ltd in Ireland is also the manufacturer and supplier of the Filshie Clip System to all UTMD subsidiaries worldwide, so lower intercompany Filshie sales also contributed to the lower consolidated 3Q GPM.  In contrast, UTMD’s consolidated 9M 2026 GPM was higher than in 9M 2025, as expected, due to a more favorable product mix without $2,058 low GP sales to the former China distributor.  

 

More specifically, 3Q 2026 GP was $764 (13.6%) lower than in 3Q 2025 when sales were 12.1% lower. GP in 9M 2026 was $1,857 (11.1%) lower than in 9M 2025 when sales were 12.2% lower. The consolidated 3Q 2026 GPM was 56.1% compared to 57.1% in 3Q 2025. The consolidated 9M 2026 GPM was 57.5% compared to 56.8% in 9M 2025.    

 

Operating Income (OI)

 

OI results from subtracting Operating Expenses (OE) from GP.  OE are comprised of Sales and Marketing (S&M) expenses, General and Administrative (G&A) expenses and Product Development (R&D) expenses.

 

The lower GPM in 3Q 2026 on 12% lower sales compared to 3Q 2025 was offset by $181 lower litigation expenses and the beneficial impact of Femcare IIA amortization expense in 3Q 2026 that was $511 lower than in 3Q 2025. Consolidated OI of $2,795 was $183 (+7.0%) higher compared to 3Q 2025 OI of $2,612. UTMD’s 3Q 2026 OI margin (OI as a percentage of sales) increased to 32.4% of sales from 26.6% of sales in 3Q 2025.  

 

OI in 9M 2026 was $8,009 compared to $8,961 in 9M 2025, a $952 (10.6%) decrease, although with a higher OI margin of 31.0% compared to 30.4% in 9M 2025.  The lower 9M OI was the result of 11.1% lower GP despite a higher GPM compared to 9M 2025.

Income Before Tax (EBT)

 

EBT results from subtracting net non-operating expense (NOE) or adding net non-operating income (NOI) from or to, as applicable, OI.  Consolidated 3Q 2026 EBT was $3,401 (39.5% of sales) compared to $3,309 (33.7% of sales) in 3Q 2025. Consolidated 9M 2026 EBT was $9,870 (38.1% of sales) compared to $11,004 (37.3% of sales) in 9M 2025.      

 

NOE/NOI includes the combination of 1) expenses from loan interest and bank fees; 2) expenses or income from losses or gains from remeasuring the value of EUR cash bank balances in the UK, and GBP cash balances in Ireland, in USD terms on September 30, 2026; and 3) income from rent of underutilized property, investment income and royalties received from licensing the Company’s technology.

Starting in 2024 for UTMD, there has been an additional excise tax included in UTMD’s NOE: a stock repurchase 1% excise tax enacted in the so-called “Inflation Reduction Act of 2022”.  The price of shares repurchased in 3Q 2026 and 9M 2026 was $699 and $905 respectively. Shares repurchased in 3Q 2025 and 9M 2025 were $653 and $7,362 respectively. The cumulative excise tax on share repurchases since 2024 has been $292.

 

Net NOI is NOI minus NOE. Net NOI in 3Q 2026 was $606 compared to $698 in 3Q 2025. Net NOI in 9M 2026 was $1,860 compared to $2,043 in 9M 2025. The lower NOI in both periods was essentially due to lower interest rates. Remeasured foreign currency balances generated about $8 less in net NOE in 9M 2026 compared to 9M 2025.     

 

EBITDA is a non-US GAAP metric that measures profitability performance without factoring in effects of financing, accounting decisions regarding non-cash expenses, capital expenditures or tax environments. Management believes that this operating performance metric provides meaningful supplemental information to both management and investors and confirms UTMD’s ongoing excellent financial operating performance, as well as its ability to sustain high level financial performance during a challenging time.

 

Excluding the noncash effects of depreciation, amortization of intangible assets and stock option expense, 3Q 2026 consolidated EBT excluding the remeasured bank balance currency gain or loss (“adjusted consolidated EBITDA”) was $3,735 (43.3% of sales) compared to $4,170 (42.5% of sales) in 3Q 2025.  Adjusted consolidated EBITDA was $11,303 (43.7% of sales) in 9M 2026 compared to $13,493 (45.8% of sales) in 9M 2025. The lower EBITDA in 3Q 2026 compared to 3Q 2025 was due to the lack of IIA amortization.  The lower 9M 2026 EBITDA compared to 9M 2025 was due to lower EBT, accentuated by lower IIA amortization expense in 9M 2026. Adjusted consolidated trailing twelve months’ (TTM) EBITDA was $15,269 as of September 30, 2026.

Net Income (NI)

 

NI in 3Q 2026 of $2,787 (32.3% of sales) was 5.9% higher than the NI of $2,631 (26.8% of sales) in 3Q 2025.  The $156 higher 3Q 2026 NI was due to $183 higher OI combined with $92 lower net NOI, and a two-percentage point lower estimated average income tax provision rate. The average consolidated income tax provision rate (as a % of the same period EBT) in 3Q 2026 was 18.1% compared to 20.5% in 3Q 2025.

 

NI in 9M 2026 of $8,077 (31.2% of sales) was 7.4% lower than the NI of $8,720 (29.6% of sales) in 9M 2025.  The $643 lower 9M 2026 NI was due to $951 lower OI combined with $183 lower net NOI, and a lower estimated average income tax provision rate. The average consolidated income tax provision rate (as a % of the same period EBT) in 9M 2026 was 18.2% compared to 20.8% in 9M 2025.

The consolidated income tax provision rate varies as the mix in taxable income among U.S. and foreign subsidiaries with differing income tax rates differs from period to period. The basic corporate income tax rates in each of the sovereignties were the same as in the prior year.

The same was true for both 9M 2026 and 9M 2025. The number of shares used for calculating EPS in both years was higher than period-ending outstanding shares because of a time-weighted calculation of average outstanding shares.
