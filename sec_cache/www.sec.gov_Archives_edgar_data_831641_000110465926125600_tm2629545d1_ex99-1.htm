Tetra Tech Reports Strong Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2026 Results

 

·Fourth quarter revenue $1.34 billion and net revenue $1.15 billion

 

·Record fourth quarter EPS $0.51 and adjusted EPS $0.49

 

·Record fiscal year EPS $1.69 and adjusted EPS $1.60

 

·Backlog $4.67 billion, up 13% Y/Y

 

Pasadena, California. Tetra Tech, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTEK), a global provider of high-end technical and engineering services, Leading with Science® in water, environment and sustainable infrastructure, today announced results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year ended September 27, 2026.

 

Fourth Quarter Results

 

Revenue and revenue, net of subcontractor costs (net revenue)1, in the fourth quarter totaled $1.34 billion and $1.15 billion, respectively. Net revenue increased 5% year-over-year excluding episodic disaster response. Operating income was $188 million and adjusted EBITDA1 was $194 million; adjusted EBITDA margin increased 90 basis points year-over-year. EPS was $0.51 and adjusted EPS1 was $0.49, up 9% year-over-year. Backlog was $4.67 billion at the end of the fourth quarter, up 13% year-over-year.

 

Recent Key Wins

 

·$1.4 billion multiple-award blanket purchase agreement for enterprise logistics services for NASA

 

·$145 million multiple-award contract for engineering and technical consulting services for U.S. Navy

 

·$141 million single-award contract for water infrastructure assessment services for U.S. EPA

 

·$85 million multiple-award contracts for environmental services for USACE Europe District

 

·$63 million single-award contract for emergency preparedness and response services for U.S. EPA

 

·$49 million multiple-award contract for geospatial and engineering services for NOAA

 

·$14 million single-award contract for digital systems modernization for LA Metro

 

·$12.7 million single-award contracts for technical services for Millennium Challenge Corporation

Executive Management Comments

 

Roger Argus, Chief Executive Officer, commented, “We completed fiscal year 2026 with a strong fourth quarter driven by our global commercial and government businesses. We continue to see increasing demand for our differentiated Leading with Science® technical services for water and environmental programs, including digital automation for associated facilities. Our resilient infrastructure design services are growing globally with new contracts for water supply, coastal infrastructure, and defense modernization. These key client programs drove our backlog up by more than $530 million, an increase of 13% from last year. This strong momentum in backlog gives us confidence in continued revenue growth and further margin expansion.”

 

Steve Burdick, Chief Financial Officer, stated, “In fiscal year 2026, we continued to increase our profit margins with 90 basis points of EBITDA margin expansion from fiscal year 2025. The strength of our balance sheet and consistent cash flow generation position us to continue to invest in organic growth and strategic acquisitions.”

 

Quarterly Dividend and Share Repurchase Program

 

On November 9, 2026, Tetra Tech’s Board of Directors approved a quarterly dividend in the amount of $0.072 per share, an 11% increase year-over-year, payable on December 11, 2026, to stockholders of record as of December 2, 2026. This is the 46th consecutive double-digit increase in the Company’s quarterly dividend. In the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026, Tetra Tech repurchased $100 million of common stock. Additionally, as of September 27, 2026, the Company had $798 million remaining under its share repurchase program.

 

Fiscal Year Results

 

Revenue for fiscal year 2026 was $5.08 billion and net revenue was $4.35 billion. For fiscal year 2026, net revenue increased 7% compared to last year excluding USAID / DOS and episodic disaster response. Operating income was $619 million, adjusted EBITDA was $660 million with margin up 90 basis points compared to last year, and EPS was $1.69. Cash flows generated from operations were up 14% year-over-year.
