Upstream Bio Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Highlights Continued Progress

 

– Company plans to initiate Phase 3 trials in both severe asthma and CRSwNP in the first quarter of 2027 –

 

– Prioritizing Phase 3 development strategy designed to deliver best-in-class efficacy and convenient quarterly dosing –

 

WALTHAM, Mass. – November 10, 2026 - Upstream Bio, Inc. (Nasdaq: UPB), a clinical-stage company developing treatments for severe inflammatory respiratory diseases, today reported financial results and business updates for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Company highlighted its continued progress in developing verekitug to deliver potential best-in-class efficacy with quarterly dosing across three target indications: severe asthma, chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps (CRSwNP), and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). Verekitug is the only known antagonist currently in clinical development that targets and inhibits the thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP) receptor.

“Our team is focused on the timely execution of startup activities for verekitug’s Phase 3 trials in severe asthma and CRSwNP. Preparations for the planned initiation of these trials in the first quarter of 2027 are well underway, with key clinical, regulatory, and manufacturing milestones now having been achieved,” said Rand Sutherland, MD, Chief Executive Officer of Upstream Bio. “With the substantial and consistent clinical evidence generated across our programs to date, we believe that verekitug has the potential to make a meaningful difference for patients living with these diseases. As we look ahead, we remain focused on advancing verekitug in diseases with significant remaining unmet medical need, where we believe its compelling efficacy profile and convenient quarterly at-home dosing have the opportunity to meaningfully redefine treatment expectations and establish a new standard of care.”

Additional Business Highlights

•
Trials in COPD and Severe Asthma Long-Term Extension (LTE) Ongoing
o
The Phase 2 VENTURE trial in COPD (NCT06981078) remains ongoing with approximately 480 participants enrolled. VENTURE is a global, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group clinical trial designed to assess the efficacy and safety of verekitug in adults with moderate-to-severe COPD across extended dosing-interval arms of 12 and 24 weeks, with a primary endpoint of annualized COPD exacerbation rates. Enrollment completion was announced in August 2026 and data from the study are expected in the second half of 2027. The Company believes that this trial will provide important insights into the potential clinical impact of verekitug in COPD.

 

o
The Phase 2 VALOUR (NCT06966479) LTE study in severe asthma also remains ongoing, with more than 90% retention of eligible participants from the Phase 2 VALIANT study. VALOUR is a dose-blinded, active treatment extension study for eligible participants with severe asthma who completed the Phase 2 VALIANT study to assess the long-term efficacy and safety outcomes of verekitug. Data from the study are expected in the second half of 2027.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

As of September 30, 2026, Upstream Bio had cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $207.4 million, which is expected to fund planned operations through 2027.

 

 

Research and development expenses were $36.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $33.0 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $3.6 million was primarily driven by an increase in personnel-related expenses, including share-based compensation, and an increase in clinical costs related to the Company’s verekitug programs.

General and administrative expenses were $7.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $5.5 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $1.7 million was primarily driven by an increase in personnel-related expenses, including share-based compensation, and an increase in professional fees.

Net loss was $41.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $33.7 million for the same period in 2025.

About Upstream Bio
Upstream Bio is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing treatments for severe inflammatory respiratory diseases. The Company is developing verekitug, the only known antagonist currently in clinical development that targets and inhibits the receptor for thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP), a cytokine which is a clinically validated driver of inflammatory response positioned upstream of multiple inflammatory pathways. With more than 500 participants treated with verekitug across its clinical development programs and positive Phase 2 results in chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps (CRSwNP) and severe asthma, verekitug has a substantial body of clinical evidence supporting its advancement into Phase 3 development in both diseases. Verekitug is also being evaluated in an ongoing Phase 2 trial in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). Upstream Bio is focused on leveraging verekitug’s differentiated mechanism, potency, and potential for extended dosing to develop a treatment which may deliver best-in-class efficacy and quarterly dosing for patients underserved by today’s standard of care.
