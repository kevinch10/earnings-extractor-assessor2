HeartCore Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

NEW YORK and TOKYO, November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – HeartCore Enterprises, Inc. (Nasdaq: HTCR) (“HeartCore” or the “Company”), an IPO consulting services company based in Tokyo, reported financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Recent Operational Highlights

 

●Entered into a non-exclusive business development and sales agency agreement in Japan with ONE AI, Co., Ltd. (“ONE AI”)
●As of September 30, 2026, HeartCore was engaged with 16 Go IPO clients, including 6 clients currently in various stages of preparation for potential initial public offerings and U.S. exchange listings.
●Announced and completed the transfer of the Company’s 51% equity interest in HeartCore Luvina Vietnam Company Limited (“HeartCore Luvina”) to Luvina Software Joint Stock Company for JPY 29,000,000 (approximately $184,000). Together with the June 2026 divestiture of Sigmaways, Inc., the transaction concludes the Company’s exit from software development services and concentrates its operations on Go IPO consulting, capital markets advisory, and financial services.

 

Management Commentary

 

HeartCore CEO Sumitaka Kanno commented: “With the transfer of our interest in HeartCore Luvina completed during the third quarter, we have concluded the portfolio optimization we began earlier this year. HeartCore now operates as a focused Go IPO consulting, capital markets advisory, and financial services company, which we believe allows us to direct management attention and capital toward the areas where we see the greatest long-term opportunity.

 

“The Nasdaq listing environment remains selective, and a number of our clients have extended their IPO timelines as they work to satisfy stricter exchange and regulatory requirements. We continue to prioritize clients that we believe demonstrate stronger fundamentals, organizational readiness, and a clear path to a U.S. listing, and we are cautiously optimistic that this disciplined approach will support a higher-quality pipeline over time.

 

“Beyond our core business, just last week, we announced a new business development and sales agency agreement with ONE AI. Under this agreement, we will support ONE AI’s commercial expansion into the Japanese robotics market. Importantly, the agreement introduces an additional commission-based revenue stream, further diversifying our revenue base as we continue to navigate the current U.S. listing environment.

 

“Looking ahead, we intend to maintain disciplined cost management as we work to convert our pipeline into new engagements and listing milestones. With a simpler structure in place, we expect our results going forward to more clearly reflect the performance of our core business.”

 

 
 

 

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Revenues were $162,000 compared to $1.2 million in the same period last year. The decrease was primarily due to fewer Go IPO consulting clients and fewer successful client IPOs during the period.

 

Gross profit was $16,000 compared to $1.1 million in the same period last year. The decrease reflected lower Go IPO consulting revenues and higher consulting service costs, including increased outsourcing fees.

 

Operating expenses were $1.2 million compared to $899,000 in the same period last year. The increase was primarily due to higher general and administrative expenses, including salaries and benefits, restructuring-related consulting and professional fees, and rent, partially offset by lower selling expenses.

 

Net loss was $596,000 compared to net income of $351,000 in the same period last year. The change reflected lower gross profit, higher operating expenses, and a shift from income to a loss from discontinued operations, partially offset by net other income and an income tax benefit.

 

Adjusted EBITDA was $(1.1) million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $0.4 million in the same period last year.

 

Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Results

 

Revenues were $270,000 compared to $1.7 million in the same period last year. The decrease was primarily due to fewer Go IPO consulting clients and fewer successful client IPOs during the period.

 

Gross loss was $260,000 compared to gross profit of $1.3 million in the same period last year. The change reflected lower Go IPO consulting revenues and higher service costs, including increased resources devoted to client service and higher outsourcing fees.

 

Operating expenses were $3.1 million compared to $2.8 million in the same period last year. The increase was primarily due to higher general and administrative expenses, including salaries and benefits, rent, and restructuring-related consulting and professional fees, partially offset by lower selling expenses and reductions in certain other administrative costs.

 

Net loss was $3.8 million compared to a net loss of $1.7 million in the same period last year. The increase reflected a shift from gross profit to gross loss, higher operating expenses, and a loss from discontinued operations compared to income in the prior-year period, partially offset by lower net other expenses and an income tax benefit.

 

Adjusted EBITDA was $(3.2) million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $(0.8) million in the same period last year.

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash and cash equivalents of $1.0 million compared to $1.5 million as of December 31, 2025.

 

 
 

 

 

About HeartCore Enterprises, Inc.

 

HeartCore Enterprises, Inc. is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan, and is a leading consulting services company providing U.S. market listing support and related advisory services primarily to Japanese corporate clients. For more information, please visit https://heartcore-enterprises.com/.

 

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

This document includes references to Adjusted EBITDA, which is a non-GAAP financial measure. For the purposes of this presentation, Adjusted EBITDA is calculated by adjusting net loss to exclude depreciation and amortization, changes in fair value of investments in marketable securities, changes in fair value of investment in warrants, changes in fair value of derivative liability, interest income, interest expenses, other income, and other expenses.

 

This measure is presented as supplemental information and is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the U.S. (“GAAP”).

 

Management believes that Adjusted EBITDA provides useful information to investors by highlighting the Company’s core operational performance, excluding non-cash and non-recurring items. However, non-GAAP financial measures have limitations and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for financial results prepared in accordance with GAAP.
