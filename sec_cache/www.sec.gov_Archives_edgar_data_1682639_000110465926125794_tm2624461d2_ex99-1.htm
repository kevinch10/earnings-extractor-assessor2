HYPD Investment Thesis & 15-Month Trailing Results

 

In June of 2025, Hyperion DeFi evolved its operating strategy to the acquisition of HYPE and directly building in the Hyperliquid ecosystem. We made a firm commitment that we would be more than just HYPE, benefitting from not just holding the asset, but uniquely building multiple business atop it that have the potential to maximize its profitability. Our model is no longer a concept that we ask investors to envision, it has become reality.

 

Our unique strategy shares in multiple frontiers of value creation at the same time. Those three frontiers include (1) Asset Value – our HYPE Treasury, (2) Operating Value – our DeFi Businesses, and (3) Option Value – our embedded economic upside in the Hyperliquid ecosystem. We call this our HYPD “Triple-Dip” Strategy, and we believe our results over the past fifteen months speak for themselves.

 

·Growing HYPE Treasury: From June 2025 to September 2026, our Gross HYPE Tokens(2) have increased 41% from 1.31 million to 1.85 million HYPE Tokens, even after selling HYPE in Q3 to help retire our legacy debt. Over the same period, our Net Asset Value(9) has grown 338% from $41.6 million to $182.4 million.

 

·Five Scalable Businesses: From Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, our quarterly Adjusted Gross Profit(1) has grown 536% from $0.4 million to $2.8 million as our DeFi businesses have ramped. We have earned more than 2x, and as much as 3.6x, base HYPE staking yield in each of the last four quarters.

 

·Embedded Economic Upside in the Hyperliquid Ecosystem: We have received tokens, equity, or future rights to tokens or equity in multiple early-stage builders and infrastructure partners on Hyperliquid: Kinetiq, HyperLend, Silhouette, Skew, and DoubleZero. In September, we also became entitled to an allocation of DOMA tokens through our work with D3, and expect to continue providing ecosystem access to HYPD holders.

 

·Declining Cost Base: From Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, our Operating Expenses Excluding Stock-Based Compensation(5) declined 48% from $4.3 million to $2.2 million, and Core Operating Earnings(19) swung from a loss of $3.9 million to positive $0.6 million. Our legacy biotech segment has been wound down, and in July we sold all remaining Optejet IP to Arctic Vision.

 

·Improving Cash Flows: From Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, quarterly Adjusted Net Operating Cash Flow(18) has improved from ($2.8 million) to positive $0.1 million, both as a function of reduced costs and ramping DeFi businesses. We delivered positive Adjusted Net Operating Cash Flow(18) in Q3, ahead of our year-end target.

 

·Optimizing Capital Structure: In Q3 we repaid all outstanding principal and interest on our Avenue Capital loan (approximately $8.6 million), retiring all of our legacy debt, and we have begun repurchasing our shares under the $20 million buyback program announced on September 8. We ended Q3 with $14.5 million of cash, cash equivalents, and stablecoins(16) and no long-term debt outstanding.

 

·Guidance: We are reiterating our anticipated $7 million to $8 million Adjusted Gross Profit(1) in 2026, approximately 6x our 2025 FY results.

Adjusted Gross Profit(1) in Q3’26 and Q2’26

 

Adjusted Gross Profit(1), a Non-GAAP Metric, aims to capture all of Hyperion DeFi’s value-add operating business activities beyond gains and losses in our digital asset treasury. In total, Adjusted Gross Profit(1) increased +143% quarter-over-quarter to $2.80 million in Q3’26 from $1.15 million in Q2’26, above the $2.0 million to $2.5 million range we guided to on September 8, 2026. Excluding Ecosystem Rewards, which can vary materially quarter-to-quarter, Adjusted Gross Profit(1) grew 45% quarter-over-quarter, and we earned 3.6x base staking yield in Q3’26. Four of our five segments grew quarter-over-quarter, and four of five achieved all-time company highs over the past 5 quarters. Below is a summary of all five of our operating business activities included within Adjusted Gross Profit(1) in these periods:

 

1.Staking Yield: We stake our HYPE to our Validator and earn rewards.

 

oOn a dollar basis, our HYPE earned from staking (10,313 HYPE tokens) generated $785 thousand Adjusted Gross Profit(1) in Q3’26 versus $527 thousand in Q2’26 (+49% quarter-over-quarter), largely driven by an increase in the Effective Average HYPE Price In-Period(3) to 75.9 in Q3’26 from 51.2 in Q2’26.

 

2.Validator Commissions: The Company operates its Validator under a Joint Validator Operators Agreement (together with Kinetiq and MAVAN) and earns commissions on rewards delivered to third-party tokens delegated to the Validator.

 

oOn a dollar basis, our HYPE earned from validator commissions (572 HYPE tokens) generated $41 thousand Adjusted Gross Profit(1) in Q3’26 versus $42 thousand in Q2’26 (roughly flat quarter-over-quarter).

 

oApproximately 6.3 million HYPE tokens were delegated to our Validator as of October 31, 2026(2).

 

oBlockdaemon and Gemini have both chosen our validator for delegated HYPE staking through their respective platforms.

 

oWe continue to see multiple opportunities for validator growth, driven by the products and services we are creating plus demand from institutions for world-class validator infrastructure.

 

oIn total, the Company earned approximately 10.9 thousand HYPE tokens from staking and validating activities in Q3’26, roughly in line with 11.1 thousand in Q2’26(2).

 

3.Yield Enhancement: The Company pursues accretive strategies to enhance yield earned on its tokens.

 

oYield Enhancement activities generated $381 thousand Adjusted Gross Profit(1) in Q3’26 versus $334 thousand in Q2’26 (+14% quarter-over-quarter).

 

oQ3’26 and Q2’26 Yield Enhancement activities primarily monetized HYPE volatility through multiple strategies OTC and on-chain, including within our Institutional Volatility Income (IVI) Vault in partnership with the Rysk protocol.

 

oWe are also earning Rysk points from our vault activity and may become eligible for future Rysk tokens, while building the infrastructure to accommodate third-party execution and integrations within Rysk Premium in the future.

4.DeFi Monetization: The Company supports and monetizes Hyperliquid DeFi activity with sustainable, scalable practices.

 

oQuarterly Results: DeFi Monetization activity generated $334 thousand Adjusted Gross Profit(1) in Q3’26, an increase of +112% versus $158 thousand in Q2’26 and a record for the company.

 

oHAUS Redeployment: As previously disclosed, the sunset of the USDH stablecoin in June 2026 opened up 800,000 of our HYPE tokens to be redeployed. In our last earnings release we noted that we had redeployed 1 million HYPE tokens into new HAUS partnerships since June 2026, including Entropy (HIP-3) and Skew (HIP-4), and that we expected those new deployments over time to position us better than before. We have now proven this out in Q3’26, with DeFi Monetization achieving a record for the company.

 

oData Services: On September 24, 2026, we helped DoubleZero launch the fastest commercially available Hyperliquid data feeds, which gives us a new Data Services business on our Validator Operations that earns future revenue from publishing Hyperliquid market data.

 

oHAUS-Skew (HIP-4): Our HAUS agreement with Skew Technologies allocates 500,000 of our staked HYPE to support the deployment of HIP-4 outcome markets. The Skew team has put their markets RFQ into production, incorporating feedback from Hyperliquid that both improves capital efficiency for market makers on their platform and enables multi-leg orders for options and parlays. We expect this to simplify participation in outcome markets on Hyperliquid, and see growing interest across Partner Markets, distribution agreements and multiple market maker onboardings in the immediate pipeline.

 

oHAUS-Entropy (HIP-3): In August 2026 we announced a HAUS partnership with Entropy with 500,000 of our staked HYPE. Our deal with Entropy supports HIP-3 perpetuals futures markets, which have seen strong growth over the past three months, with over $3B of volume traded and daily open interest above $80M. Pre-IPO trading for Anthropic has been their most successful market, and a testament to their unique market design methodology for enabling the asset to trade prior to Anthropic’s upcoming IPO. They currently maintain the second largest share of Hyperliquid’s HIP-3 markets and we expect them to release additional updates over the coming months that enhance the accessibility of their markets.

 

oHAUS-Silhouette (Trading Fee Reduction): In March 2026 we launched a HAUS agreement on 100,000 HYPE tokens with Silhouette, whereby Silhouette receives reduced trading fees for their clients and we receive a portion of those savings as revenue. Silhouette (which provides block and shielded trading on Hyperliquid to its clients) completed its migration into production in Q2’26. We saw monthly volumes step up from the hundreds of thousands toward over $110 million cumulative to date. This was driven by Silhouette’s support for RWA spot trading such as xStocks, which we expect to continue to accelerate on Hyperliquid as more assets become tokenized and move on-chain.
