SHOE STATION GROUP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

 

November 24, 2026

 

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

 

FORT MILL, SC - Shoe Station Group, Inc. (Nasdaq: SHOE) (the “Company”), a leading omnichannel retailer of footwear and accessories for the family, today reported results for the third quarter ended October 31, 2026 and updated its Fiscal 2026 guidance.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

•
Net sales of $280.0 million compared to $297.2 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•
Shoe Carnival banner net sales declined 4.7 percent; Shoe Station banner net sales declined 7.7 percent. Sales in both banners were impacted by an increasingly promotional footwear marketplace and softer consumer demand following back-to-school.
•
Gross profit margin of 31.9 percent, compared to 37.6 percent in the third quarter of 2025, reflecting increased promotional activity, liquidation of aged and excess inventory, and the prior-year benefit from raising prices in advance of tariff-related cost increases.
•
Results include an approximately $18.0 million non-cash goodwill impairment charge; the charge will not result in any cash expenditures or affect the Company’s cash flow or liquidity.
•
Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities of $116.0 million at quarter-end; the Company ended the third quarter of 2026 debt-free.
•
Repurchased approximately $10.0 million of common stock during the third quarter; year-to-date 2026 capital returned to shareholders totaled approximately $31.1 million, inclusive of dividends.
•
Full year 2026 net sales guidance and non-GAAP adjusted diluted earnings per share (“Adjusted EPS”) guidance lowered to reflect results through the third quarter and current family footwear industry trends.

“Our third quarter results were below the expectations we set in September. The improvement we saw in August did not carry into September and October. Families concentrated their spending around back-to-school, and then, as our advertising intensity lessened and fuel prices increased, consumer demand softened until cold weather arrived. Early results from our merchandising work are encouraging. Customers responded to our boot assortment as soon as the season opened, and those sales carried higher average unit retails and improved margins. Fall assortments that were curated to address localized demand began to arrive in our stores throughout the month of October,” said Cliff Sifford, Interim President and Chief Executive Officer.

 

 

“We enter the holiday season with the strongest boot assortment we have offered in years, intensified advertising, moderating fuel prices, and comparisons that ease as the quarter progresses. Our balance sheet remains strong. We are debt-free, we ended the quarter with $116 million in cash and marketable securities, and we returned capital to shareholders through our 58th consecutive quarterly dividend and the repurchase of approximately $10 million of our stock. We are managing this company for the long term, and the work underway in our assortments, our marketing, and our costs is aimed at returning this business to profitable growth,” concluded Mr. Sifford.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Operating Results

Net sales in the third quarter of 2026 were $280.0 million compared to $297.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. Comparable store sales declined 5.1 percent, with a decline of 2.7 percent in fiscal August and approximately 7 percent in each of fiscal September and fiscal October.

By banner:

•
Shoe Carnival net sales were $180.3 million, representing 64 percent of total net sales, and declined 4.7 percent, inclusive of a comparable store sales decline of 3.7 percent.
•
Shoe Station net sales were $99.3 million, representing 36 percent of total net sales, and declined 7.7 percent, inclusive of a comparable store sales decline of 7.6 percent.

Gross profit margin in the third quarter of 2026 was 31.9 percent, a decrease of 570 basis points compared to the third quarter of 2025. Merchandise margin decreased 480 basis points compared to the third quarter of 2025. Merchandise margin in the third quarter of 2025 included a temporary benefit from raising prices in advance of increasing tariff-related costs. In the third quarter of 2026, merchandise margins were also impacted by increased promotional activity and product liquidation. Buying, distribution and occupancy costs increased compared to the third quarter of 2025 and reduced gross profit margin by 90 basis points, primarily due to the deleveraging effect of lower sales.

Selling, general and administrative expenses (“SG&A”) decreased $6.2 million compared to the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to lower selling costs, including advertising and other rebanner-related expenses, and lower performance-based compensation. As a percent of net sales, SG&A for the third quarter of 2026 was 31.1 percent as compared to 31.3 percent in the third quarter of 2025.

Income tax benefit in the third quarter of 2026 was $2.1 million and the effective tax rate in the third quarter of 2026 was 14.6 percent compared to 25.5 percent in the third quarter of 2025. The Company’s non-GAAP adjusted effective tax rate (“Adjusted Tax Rate”) in the third quarter of 2026 was 63.2 percent.

The GAAP net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $(12.3) million, or $(0.46) per diluted share, compared to net income of $14.6 million, or $0.53 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Excluding the non-cash goodwill impairment charge recorded in the quarter, non-GAAP adjusted net income (“Adjusted Net Income”) and Adjusted EPS were $1.3 million and $0.05 per diluted share, respectively.

 

Goodwill Impairment

Due to the decline in the Company’s financial performance and stock price through the third quarter of 2026, a quantitative impairment assessment was performed on its reporting unit. The Company concluded that a non-cash goodwill impairment charge was required because the carrying value of its reporting unit exceeded its estimated fair value. As a result, the Company recorded a non-cash goodwill impairment charge of approximately $18.0 million in the third quarter of 2026. The impairment reflects recent operating performance and updated expectations for the business, which resulted in a reduction in the estimated fair value of the reporting unit relative to its carrying value. The impairment charge will not result in any cash expenditures and will not affect the Company’s cash flow or liquidity. Additional information regarding this impairment charge is provided in the reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP financial measures at the end of this release. Year-to-Date 2026 Operating Results

Net sales during the first nine months of 2026 (year-to-date 2026) were $835.0 million, a decrease of 5.2 percent compared to the first nine months of 2025 (year-to-date 2025). Comparable store sales declined 4.8 percent. By banner, Shoe Carnival comparable store sales declined 3.9 percent and Shoe Station comparable store sales declined 6.3 percent.

Gross profit was $270.1 million in year-to-date 2026, a decrease of $56.3 million compared to year-to-date 2025. Gross profit margin was 32.4 percent in year-to-date 2026 compared to 37.0 percent in year-to-date 2025.

SG&A decreased $4.5 million in year-to-date 2026 compared to year-to-date 2025, primarily due to lower advertising and other rebanner-related costs and lower performance-based compensation, partially offset by $13.6 million of non-recurring charges incurred in the first quarter of 2026 related to the previously disclosed Chief Executive Officer transition (the “CEO Transition”) and the Company’s strategic review of its rebanner strategy and other operations (the “Strategic Review”). As a percent of net sales, SG&A for year-to-date 2026 was 31.9 percent compared to 30.7 percent in year-to-date 2025. Year-to-date 2026 Adjusted SG&A, which excludes the $13.6 million of non-recurring charges described above, decreased $18.1 million compared to year-to-date 2025.

The Company’s effective tax rate in year-to-date 2026 was (6.8) percent compared to 26.2 percent in year-to-date 2025. The change was primarily due to the pre-tax loss position for the period and the non-deductibility of a portion of the severance paid as part of the CEO Transition. The Company’s Adjusted Tax Rate for year-to-date 2026, which excludes the impacts related to the CEO Transition, was 33.2 percent.

Year-to-date 2026 net loss was $(11.7) million, or $(0.43) per diluted share, compared to net income of $43.2 million and EPS of $1.57 in the first nine months of 2025. Year-to-date Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted EPS, which each exclude the first-quarter charges discussed above and the third-quarter non-cash goodwill impairment charge, were $13.8 million and $0.51 per diluted share, respectively.

 

Fiscal 2026 Guidance

The Company is adjusting its previously communicated Fiscal 2026 guidance, which now contemplates:

•
Net sales of $1.087 billion to $1.092 billion, representing a decline of approximately 4.3 to 3.8 percent versus Fiscal 2025, inclusive of comparable store sales for the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2026 in a range of flat to up 2 percent;
•
GAAP net loss per share of $(0.84) to $(0.76) and Adjusted EPS of $0.20 to $0.28;
•
Gross profit margin of approximately 31.4 to 31.6 percent, representing approximately 500 to 520 basis points of compression versus Fiscal 2025;
•
GAAP SG&A up approximately $5.9 million versus Fiscal 2025 and a reduction in Adjusted SG&A of approximately $11.6 million versus Fiscal 2025, inclusive of incremental advertising investment supporting the fall and holiday seasons;
•
A GAAP tax benefit rate of approximately 11 percent and an Adjusted Tax Rate of approximately 38 percent; and
•
Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities in excess of $150 million at the end of the fourth quarter.

Adjusted EPS and Adjusted Tax Rate guidance also exclude the $18.0 million non-cash goodwill impairment charge recorded during the third quarter of 2026. About Shoe Station Group

Shoe Station Group, Inc. is one of the nation’s largest omnichannel family footwear retailers, offering a broad assortment of dress, casual and athletic footwear for men, women and children with emphasis on national name brands. As of November 24, 2026, the Company operated 420 stores in 35 states and Puerto Rico under its Shoe Carnival and Shoe Station banners and offers shopping at shoecarnival.com and shoestation.com. Headquartered in Fort Mill, SC, and with distribution and support operations located in Evansville, IN, Shoe Station Group, Inc. trades on The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC under the symbol SHOE.

Press releases and annual reports are available on the Company's website at investors.shoestationgroupinc.com.

Use of Non-GAAP Measures

In this press release, to supplement amounts presented in our consolidated financial statements determined in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States ("GAAP"), the Company uses certain non-GAAP financial measures, including reduction in Adjusted SG&A, Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted EPS and Adjusted Tax Rate, as shown in the tables above. These measures adjust for the charges and corresponding impact associated with (1) the CEO Transition, including cash severance, accelerated stock-based compensation, legal fees, payroll taxes, and outplacement fees, all of which occurred in the thirteen weeks ended May 2, 2026, (2) long-lived asset impairments and write-offs in connection with the completion of the Company’s Strategic Review of its rebanner program and other operations, all of which occurred in the thirteen weeks ended May 2, 2026, and (3) non-cash impairment of goodwill in the thirteen weeks ended October 31, 2026. The Company’s Fiscal 2026 guidance measures also exclude the charges and corresponding impact associated with expenses expected to be incurred in the fourth quarter of 2026 to return certain rebannered stores to their original store configuration. The unaudited adjusted results should not be construed as an alternative to the reported results determined in accordance with GAAP. These financial measures are not based on any standardized methodology and are not necessarily comparable to similar measures presented by other companies. The Company believes that these non-GAAP financial measures provide useful information to both management and investors to increase comparability to prior periods by adjusting for certain items that may not be indicative of core operating measures and to better identify trends in the Company’s business. The adjusted financial results are used by management to, and allow investors to, evaluate the operating performance of the Company compared to prior periods, when reviewed in conjunction with the Company's GAAP statements.
