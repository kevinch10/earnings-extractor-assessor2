HOUSTON, October 28, 2026. Prosperity Bancshares, Inc.® (NYSE: PB) (“Prosperity Bancshares”), the parent company of Prosperity Bank® (collectively, “Prosperity”), reported net income of $141.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with $137.6 million for the same period in 2025. Net income per diluted common share was $1.18 for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with $1.45 for the same period in 2025. On January 1, 2026, American Bank Holding Corporation (“American”) merged into Prosperity Bancshares, and American Bank, N.A. (“American Bank”) merged into Prosperity Bank (collectively, the “American Merger”). On February 1, 2026, Southwest Bancshares, Inc. (“Southwest”) merged into Prosperity Bancshares, and Texas Partners Bank (“Texas Partners”) merged into Prosperity Bank (collectively, the “Southwest Merger”). On July 1, 2026, Stellar Bancorp, Inc. (“Stellar”) merged into Prosperity Bancshares, and Stellar Bank (“Stellar Bank”) merged into Prosperity Bank (collectively, the “Stellar Merger”, and together with the American Merger and the Southwest Merger, the “Mergers”). During the third quarter of 2026, Prosperity incurred merger related expenses of $69.7 million, or $0.46(1) per diluted common share and an out of state tax settlement of $5.0 million, or $0.04(1) per diluted common share, partially offset by a gain of $927 thousand, or $0.01(1) per diluted common share as a result of the exchange and conversion of Visa Inc. (“Visa”) Class B-2 stock. Excluding the merger related expenses, net of tax, the tax settlement, and the net gain, net of tax, net income was $200.8(1) million and net income per diluted common share was $1.67(1) for the third quarter of 2026. The annualized return on third quarter average assets was 1.06%. Nonperforming assets remained low at 0.41% of third quarter average interest-earning assets.

“I am pleased to announce that the Board of Directors approved increasing the fourth quarter 2026 dividend to $0.64 per share from the $0.60 per share that was paid in the prior four quarters, an increase of 6.7%. The increase reflects the continued confidence the Board has in our company and our markets. The compound annual growth rate in dividends declared from 2003 to 2026 was 10.4%.

Asset Quality

Nonperforming assets totaled $193.9 million or 0.41% of quarterly average interest-earning assets at September 30, 2026, compared with $119.6 million or 0.36% of quarterly average interest-earning assets at September 30, 2025 and $130.6 million or 0.34% of quarterly average interest-earning assets at June 30, 2026. The allowance for credit losses on loans was $592.0 million or 1.85% of total loans at September 30, 2026, compared with $339.6 million or 1.54% of total loans at September 30, 2025, and $382.8 million or 1.53% of total loans at June 30, 2026. Excluding Warehouse Purchase Program loans, the allowance for credit losses on loans to total loans was 1.91%(1) at September 30, 2026, compared with 1.64%(1) at September 30, 2025, and 1.61%(1) at June 30, 2026.

Net charge-offs were $4.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared with net charge-offs of $6.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, and $2.2 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026. Additionally, due to the Stellar Merger, reserves increased by Day One accounting for PCD loans of $146.1 million and Day One accounting for purchased seasoned loans (“PSLs”) of $67.4 million. For the three months ended September 30, 2026, $7.6 million of reserves on resolved PCD loans without any related charge-offs were released to the general reserve.

Net charge-offs were $47.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with net charge-offs of $12.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Net charge-offs for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, included a $37.7 million increase in net charge-offs for commercial and industrial loans. Additionally, due to the Mergers, reserves increased by Day One accounting for PCD loans of $199.5 million and Day One accounting for PSLs of $106.7 million. Further, $20.1 million of reserves on resolved PCD loans without any related charge-offs were released to the general reserve.

Prosperity Bancshares, Inc. ®

As of September 30, 2026, Prosperity Bancshares, Inc.® is a $54.101 billion Houston, Texas based regional financial holding company providing personal banking services and investments to consumers and businesses throughout Texas and Oklahoma. Founded in 1983, Prosperity believes in a community banking philosophy, taking care of customers, businesses and communities in the areas it serves by providing financial solutions to simplify everyday financial needs. In addition to offering traditional deposit and loan products, Prosperity offers digital banking solutions, credit and debit cards, mortgage services, retail brokerage services, trust and wealth management, and treasury management.

Prosperity currently operates 352 full-service banking locations: 62 in the Houston area, including The Woodlands; 40 in the South Texas area including Corpus Christi and Victoria; 60 in the Dallas/Fort Worth area; 20 in the East Texas area; 26 in the Central Texas area including Austin; 7 in the San Antonio area; 45 in the West Texas area including Lubbock, Midland, Odessa, Abilene; Amarillo and Wichita Falls; 15 in the Bryan/College Station area, 6 in the Central Oklahoma area; 8 in the Tulsa, Oklahoma area; 11 in the Central Texas area and San Antonio area doing business as Texas Partners Bank; and 52 in the Houston and Dallas areas doing business as Stellar Bank.
