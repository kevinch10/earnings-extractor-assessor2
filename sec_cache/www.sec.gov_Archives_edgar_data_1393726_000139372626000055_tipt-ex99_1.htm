TIPTREE ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

Greenwich, Connecticut – October 28, 2026– Tiptree Inc. (NASDAQ:TIPT) (“Tiptree” or the “Company”), today announced its financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

•
Entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Universal Shield Insurance Group ("USIG"), a specialty property and casualty insurer, for $100.0 million in cash, adjusted for certain closing costs. The transaction remains subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions and is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027.
•
As of September 30, 2026, Tiptree's book value increased to $903 million, or $24.47 per share ($23.99 per diluted share).
•
Through October 28, 2026, repurchased $25.0 million of common stock in 2026 at an average 30% discount to book value per share.
•
Declared a dividend of $0.06 per share to stockholders of record on November 16, 2026 with a payment date of November 23, 2026.
•
Tiptree management is highly aligned with shareholders and focused on long-term value creation through strategic insurance investments, opportunistic share buybacks, and thoughtful consideration of dividends. With a disciplined financial approach, the Company continues to streamline operations and manage costs to support sustainable growth.

About Tiptree

Tiptree Inc. (NASDAQ: TIPT) is an insurance holding company focused on building and growing P&C insurance businesses in specialized markets. The Company’s strategy combines experienced management teams, disciplined underwriting and prudent investment and capital management to create long-term shareholder value. Tiptree seeks to build businesses distinguished by underwriting expertise, strong distribution relationships and sustainable profitability, with the objective of compounding book value per share over time. For more information, please visit tiptreeinc.com and follow us on LinkedIn.
