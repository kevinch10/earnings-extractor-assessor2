TEAM, INC. REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
Team Inc. Advances Operational Transformation and Commercial Diversification Initiatives.

SUGAR LAND, TX – November 11, 2026 – Team, Inc. (NYSE: TISI) (“TEAM” or the “Company”), a global, leading provider of specialty industrial services offering customers access to a full suite of conventional, specialized, and proprietary inspection, heat-treating, and mechanical services, today reported its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Company also updated guidance for the full year 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights:

•Reported revenue of $220.4 million, a 2.0% decrease from $225.0 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•Generated gross margin of $55.8 million, compared to $58.0 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•Reported a net loss of $11.1 million, an improvement of $0.3 million, or 3.3%, compared to a net loss of $11.4 million in the prior-year period.
•Delivered Adjusted EBITDA1 of $15.7 million (7.1% of consolidated revenue), representing an increase of $1.2 million, or 8.2%, compared to $14.5 million (6.5% of consolidated revenue) in the third quarter of 2025.
•Reduced Adjusted Selling, General and Administrative Expense¹ by $3.5 million, or 7.4%, to $43.3 million from the prior year period.
•Updated full-year guidance, including Adjusted EBITDA of $56 million to $59 million.

1 See the accompanying reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures at the end of this earnings release.

“In the third quarter, we continued to advance the key priorities of TEAM’s operational and commercial transformation as we work towards and capture early success in building a stronger, more efficient and diversified business,” said Gary L. Hill, TEAM’s Chief Executive Officer. “We are making meaningful progress on initiatives to optimize our operating footprint, improve execution and reduce our overall cost structure, while actively strengthening our commercial capabilities and expanding into higher-value industrial end markets. These efforts remain on track, and while the impacts continue to support our results in real-time, we expect the benefits of our ongoing transformation to become increasingly evident in our results as we move into 2027.”

Hill continued, “Third-quarter results continued to reflect the deferral of planned turnaround and maintenance activity across our core refining and petrochemical markets, particularly within our higher-margin mechanical services business. While demand for our inspection services has

Importantly, we believe much of this work has been deferred rather than lost, and we expect it to begin returning in early 2027, although the timing remains dependent on customer operating decisions. As we look ahead, we remain confident in the direction of our transformation and believe our expanding commercial opportunities, combined with a leaner operating structure and the eventual recovery in turnaround activity, will support stronger earnings power and cash flow generation over time.”
Financial Results

Third quarter revenue was $220.4 million, compared to $225.0 million in the prior-year period, as growth in our international markets was offset by lower revenue in the United States, while revenue in Canada remained relatively flat. Revenue increased by 8.3% in our international markets and declined 4.4% in the United States. Third quarter consolidated gross margin was $55.8 million, or 25.3% of revenue.

Selling, general and administrative expenses for the third quarter were $50.7 million, a decrease of $0.5 million or 1.1%, from the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted Selling, General and Administrative Expense, which excludes certain items not indicative of TEAM’s ongoing operations, including non-recurring professional and legal costs, transformation and severance expenses, as well as non-cash share-based compensation expense, decreased by $3.5 million, or 7.4%, to $43.3 million compared to the prior-year period.

Operating loss was $0.5 million, compared to operating income of $1.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net loss was $11.1 million, an improvement of $0.3 million compared to a net loss of $11.4 million in the prior-year period. Net loss attributable to common shareholders, which includes dividends and accretion related to the preferred shares, totaled $14.2 million, or $3.10 per share, compared to a net loss attributable to common shareholders of $12.1 million, or $2.68 per share, in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, increased to $15.7 million (7.1% of consolidated revenue), compared to $14.5 million (6.5% of consolidated revenue) in the prior-year quarter.

Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Loss, Adjusted EBIT, and Adjusted Selling, General and Administrative Expense are non-GAAP financial measures that exclude certain items that are not indicative of TEAM’s core operating activities. Reconciliations of these non-GAAP financial measures to their most directly comparable GAAP financial measures are included at the end of this earnings release.

Balance Sheet and Liquidity

At September 30, 2026, the Company had total liquidity of $43.5 million, including $8.1 million of cash and cash equivalents (excluding $2.9 million of restricted cash) and $35.4 million of available capacity under the ABL Credit Facility.

The Company’s total debt as of September 30, 2026 was $319.1 million compared to $297.2 million as of December 31, 2025. The increase is primarily due to higher net borrowings under the Company’s ABL credit facility. The Company’s Net Debt (total debt less cash and cash equivalents, including restricted cash of $2.9 million), a non-GAAP financial measure, was $308.1 million at September 30, 2026.

Updated 2026 Outlook

For fiscal year 2026, the Company is updating its operating and cash flow guidance as follows:

•Total Company Revenue of $870 million to $890 million, a decrease of approximately 2% at the midpoint of guidance compared to 2025;
•Gross Margin of between $226 million and $249 million, an increase of approximately 3% at the midpoint of guidance compared to 2025;
•Adjusted EBITDA of between $56 million and $59 million, a decrease of approximately 5% at the midpoint of guidance compared to 2025;
•Capital expenditures of between $13 million and $14 million;
•Structural cost improvements of $15 million to $18 million;
•Full program goals of $30 million to $38 million at full annual run rate.

Clinton Roeder, Chief Financial Officer commented, “TEAM’s year-to-date performance, particularly our revenues, has continued to reflect deferred turnaround and maintenance activity across our core markets, with the timing of this work remaining difficult to predict. Our updated full-year 2026 guidance reflects our current expectation that the recovery in this activity, further delayed by disruptions in the Middle East, will largely shift into early 2027. The difference compared to our 2025 results and our original 2026 guidance stems directly from these deferrals. Despite these near-term headwinds, we continue to make meaningful progress on our structural cost improvement initiatives, while maintaining a disciplined focus on working capital and cash conversion. As we move into 2027, we expect these initiatives to provide a stronger foundation for improved profitability and cash flow generation as deferred maintenance activity returns and our commercial diversification efforts gain traction.”

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
(Unaudited)

The Company uses supplemental non-GAAP financial measures derived from consolidated financial information, including Adjusted Net Income (Loss), Adjusted Net Income (Loss) per Share, Adjusted EBIT, Adjusted EBITDA, Free Cash Flow and Net Debt to supplement financial information presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”).

The Company defines Adjusted Net Income (Loss) and Adjusted Net Income (Loss) per Share to exclude the following items: non-routine legal costs and settlements, non-routine professional fees, loss on debt extinguishment, certain severance charges, incremental non-recurring transformation costs associated with workforce reductions, facility consolidations, lease termination activities and other business transformation initiatives, non-routine write-offs of assets, and certain other items that we believe are not indicative of core operating activities. Consolidated Adjusted EBIT excludes the costs excluded from Adjusted Net Income (Loss), as well as income tax expense (benefit), interest charges, foreign currency (gain) loss, pension credit, and items of other (income) expense. Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA further excludes depreciation, amortization and non-cash share-based compensation costs. Segment Adjusted EBIT is equal to segment operating income (loss) excluding costs associated with non-routine legal costs and settlements, non-routine professional fees, certain severance charges and transformation costs, and certain other items as determined by management. Segment Adjusted EBITDA further excludes depreciation, amortization, and non-cash share-based compensation costs from segment Adjusted EBIT. Adjusted Selling, General and Administrative Expense is defined to exclude non-routine legal costs and settlements, non-routine professional fees, certain severance charges and transformation costs, certain other items that we believe are not indicative of core operating activities and non-cash share-based compensation. Free Cash Flow is defined as net cash provided by (used in) operating activities minus capital expenditures paid in cash. Net Debt is defined as the sum of the current and long-term portions of debt, including finance lease obligations, less cash and cash equivalents.

Management believes these non-GAAP financial measures are useful to both management and investors in their analysis of the Company’s financial position and results of operations. In particular, Adjusted Net Income (Loss), Adjusted Net Income (Loss) per Share, consolidated Adjusted EBIT, and consolidated Adjusted EBITDA are meaningful measures of performance that are commonly used by industry analysts, investors, lenders, and rating agencies to analyze operating performance in our industry, perform analytical comparisons, benchmark performance between periods, and measure our performance against externally communicated targets. Segment Adjusted EBITDA is also used as a basis for the Chief Operating Decision Maker (Chief Executive Officer) to evaluate the performance of the Company’s reportable segments. Free Cash Flow is used by our management and investors to analyze the Company’s ability to service and repay debt and return value directly to stakeholders.

Non-GAAP measures have limitations as analytical tools, because they exclude some, but not all, items that affect net earnings and operating income. These measures should not be considered substitutes for their most directly comparable U.S. GAAP financial measures and should be considered only in conjunction with financial information presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Further, our non-GAAP financial measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies that may calculate non-GAAP financial measures differently, limiting the usefulness of those measures for comparative purposes. The liquidity measure of Free Cash Flow does not represent a precise calculation of residual cash flow available for discretionary expenditures.
