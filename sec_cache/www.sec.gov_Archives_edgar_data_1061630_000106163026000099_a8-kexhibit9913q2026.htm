Blackstone Mortgage Trust Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Results

New York, October 29, 2026 -- Blackstone Mortgage Trust, Inc. (NYSE: BXMT) today reported its third-quarter 2026 results. The net loss attributable to Blackstone Mortgage Trust for the quarter was $327 million. Third-quarter EPS, Distributable EPS, Distributable EPS prior to realized gains and losses, and dividends paid per basic share were $(1.95), $0.38, $0.42, and $0.47 respectively.

In addition, BXMT declared a dividend of $0.30 per share of class A common stock with respect to the fourth quarter of 2026. This dividend is payable on January 15, 2027, to stockholders of record as of December 31, 2026.

Tim Johnson, Chief Executive Officer said, “BXMT remains focused on balancing current return with long-term earnings power through disciplined capital allocation. We assess the dividend with our Board each quarter, considering a variety of factors, and our Board has declared a fourth quarter dividend of $0.30 per share, which we believe can be sustained through a range of rate and credit environments. With a stable balance sheet, strong liquidity, and access to the breadth and scale of Blackstone’s global platform, BXMT is well-positioned to continue to drive further portfolio turnover and dynamically allocate capital to deliver strong risk-adjusted returns for shareholders.”

Blackstone Mortgage Trust issued a full presentation of its third-quarter 2026 results, which can be viewed at www.bxmt.com. An updated investor presentation may also be viewed on the website.

About Blackstone Mortgage Trust
Blackstone Mortgage Trust (NYSE: BXMT) is a real estate finance company that originates, acquires and manages senior loans and other debt or credit-oriented investments collateralized by or relating to commercial real estate in North America, Europe, and Australia. Our investment objective is to preserve and protect shareholder capital while producing attractive risk-adjusted returns primarily through dividends generated from current income. Our portfolio is composed primarily of loans secured by high-quality, institutional assets in major markets, sponsored by experienced, well-capitalized real estate investment owners and operators. These loans are financed in a variety of ways, depending on our view of the most prudent strategy available for each of our investments. We are externally managed by BXMT Advisors L.L.C., a subsidiary of Blackstone. Further information is available at ww.bxmt.com.
