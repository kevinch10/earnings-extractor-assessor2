Twist Bioscience Reports Fiscal Fourth Quarter and Full Year Fiscal 2026 Financial Results

— Record revenue of $459.4 million in Fiscal 2026; Increase of 22% over $376.6 million in Fiscal 2025 —

— Record revenue of $126.6 million in 4QFY26; Increase of approximately 28% over $99.0 million in 4QFY25; 15th consecutive quarter of sequential growth —

— Gross margin of 52.4% in fiscal 2026, Improvement of 1.7 margin points versus 50.7% in fiscal 2025 —

— Fiscal 2027 revenue guidance of $550M-$560M, growth of 21% year over year at the midpoint —

— First quarter fiscal 2027 revenue guidance of $130M to $132M, growth of 26% year over year at the midpoint —

SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, Calif. -- (November 12, 2026) — Twist Bioscience Corporation (NASDAQ: TWST), a mid-cap growth and value healthcare company, today reported financial results and business highlights for the fourth quarter and full year fiscal 2026 ended September 30, 2026.

“Fiscal 2026 demonstrated what Twist can deliver: strong top line revenue growth, expanded gross margin and positive adjusted EBITDA in the fourth quarter, all while continuing to broaden our capabilities and strengthen our balance sheet," said Emily M. Leproust, Ph.D., CEO and co-founder of Twist Bioscience. “We enter fiscal 2027 with a stronger business and the capital to invest in our next phase of growth. AI is expanding what scientists can design, and personalized medicine is increasing the demand for unique molecules. Both play directly to our strength of customization at scale. We are building the infrastructure for where this industry is going, investing in capacity, automation and new products that enable customers to do more with Twist. Our ambition is to make Twist indispensable to the next generation of discovery, diagnostics and therapeutics.”

See "Non-GAAP Information" below and the associated reconciliation table included in this release for a discussion of the measure adjusted EBITDA.

FISCAL 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS

•Revenue: Total revenues for fiscal 2026 grew to $459.4 million compared to $376.6 million for fiscal 2025.
◦DNA Synthesis and Protein Solutions (DSPS) revenue grew 31.7% to $221.9 million for fiscal 2026 compared to $168.5 million for fiscal 2025.


◦NGS Applications (NGS) revenue grew 14.1% to $237.5 million for fiscal 2026 compared to $208.1 million for fiscal 2025.
•Cost of Revenues: Cost of revenues for fiscal 2026 was $218.6 million compared to $185.6 million for fiscal 2025.
•Gross Margin: Gross margin for fiscal 2026 increased to 52.4% compared to 50.7% for fiscal 2025.
•Research and Development Expenses: Research and development expenses for fiscal 2026 decreased to $71.8 million compared to $80.3 million for fiscal 2025.
•Selling, General and Administrative Expenses: Selling, general and administrative expenses for fiscal 2026 were $301.2 million compared to $247.0 million for fiscal 2025.
•Net Loss: Net loss for fiscal 2026 was $130.2 million, or $2.08 per diluted share, compared to net loss of $77.7 million, or $1.30 per diluted share, for fiscal 2025.
•Adjusted EBITDA: Adjusted EBITDA for fiscal 2026 was $(35.9) million compared to $(46.9) million in fiscal 2025. See the table included in this release for a reconciliation between adjusted EBITDA and net (loss) income attributable to common stockholders, the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure.
•Cash Position: As of September 30, 2026, the company had approximately $582 million in cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments.

FISCAL 2026 FOURTH QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS

•Revenue: Total revenues for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 grew 28% to $126.6 million compared to $99.0 million for the same period of fiscal 2025.
◦DNA Synthesis and Protein Solutions (DSPS) revenue grew 33% to $61.0 million for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 compared to $46.0 million for the same period of fiscal 2025, and grew 8% sequentially, compared to $56.6 million for the third quarter of fiscal 2026.
◦NGS Applications (NGS) revenue grew 24% to $65.7 million for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 compared to $53.0 million for the same period of fiscal 2025, and grew 6% sequentially, compared to $61.8 million for the third quarter of fiscal 2026.
•Cost of Revenues: Cost of revenues for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 increased to $59.4 million compared to $48.2 million for the same period of fiscal 2025.
•Gross Margin: Gross margin for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 was 53.1%, up from 51.3% for the same period of fiscal 2025.
•Research and Development Expenses: Research and development expenses for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 were $17.0 million consistent with $17.0 million for the same period of fiscal 2025.
•Selling, General and Administrative Expenses: Selling, general and administrative expenses for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 were $74.7 million compared to $63.8 million for the same period of fiscal 2025.


•Net Loss: Net loss for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 decreased to $20.6 million, or $0.32 per diluted share, compared to net loss of $27.1 million, or $0.45 per diluted share, for the same period of fiscal 2025.
•Adjusted EBITDA: Adjusted EBITDA for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 was $2.2 million compared to $(7.8) million for the same period of fiscal 2025. See the table included in this release for a reconciliation between adjusted EBITDA and net (loss) income attributable to common stockholders, the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure.

Recent Highlights:

•Shipped products to approximately 4,100 customers in fiscal 2026, versus approximately 3,800 in fiscal 2025.
•Physically shipped approximately 1.3 million genes in fiscal 2026, compared with approximately 938,000 in fiscal 2025.
•Raised net proceeds of approximately $425 million through an equity offering and at-the-market sales.
•Joined Lilly TuneLab as a provider of antibody characterization services.
•Expanded antibody production and characterization portfolio with the launch of Twist Express Screening Antibodies.
•Launched Twist MRD Express, custom panels designed for a rapid turnaround time of as little as one day.


Fiscal 2027 Financial Guidance

The following statements are based on Twist’s current expectations for fiscal 2027. The following statements are forward-looking, and actual results could differ materially depending on market conditions and the factors set forth under “Forward-Looking Statements” below.

For the full fiscal year 2027, Twist expects:

•Total revenue in the range of approximately $550 million to $560 million, growth of approximately 21% year over year at the midpoint.
◦Initial full year revenue guidance assumes $100 million in revenue for AI drug discovery and sequential growth across both DNA Synthesis and Protein Solutions and NGS Applications
•Gross margin of approximately 52.5% to 53%.
•Adjusted EBITDA positive
•Capital expenditures of approximately $75 million as part of our two-year investment plan, excluding landlord-funded tenant improvements

For the first quarter of fiscal 2027, Twist expects:



•Total revenue of approximately $130 million to $132 million, growth of approximately 26% year over year at the midpoint and continued sequential growth

Non-GAAP Information

This release includes adjusted EBITDA, which is a non-GAAP financial measure, for the periods presented. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net (loss) income adjusted to exclude interest income, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization, litigation settlement costs, net of recoveries, gain on sale of business, other income/expense, net, and stock-based compensation expense. This non-GAAP measure is not in accordance with, or an alternative for, measures prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) and may be different from non-GAAP measures used by other companies. In addition, this non-GAAP measure is not based on any comprehensive set of accounting rules or principles. We believe that this non-GAAP financial measure, when considered together with our financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP, can enhance investors’ and analysts’ ability to meaningfully compare our results from period to period and to our forward-looking guidance, and to identify operating trends in our business. However, non-GAAP information is not superior to financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP, is presented for supplemental informational purposes only, has limitations as an analytical tool and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for financial information presented in accordance with GAAP. A reconciliation table of the most comparable GAAP financial measure to the non-GAAP financial measure is included at the end of this press release.

A reconciliation of adjusted EBITDA for the full year fiscal 2027 to a corresponding GAAP financial guidance measure is not available on a forward-looking basis because the Company does not provide guidance on GAAP net (loss) income and is not able to present the various reconciling cash and non-cash items between GAAP net (loss) income and adjusted EBITDA without unreasonable effort. In particular, stock-based compensation expense is impacted by the Company’s future hiring and retention needs, as well as the future fair market value of its common stock, all of which is difficult to predict and is subject to change. The actual amount of these expenses during the full year fiscal 2027 will have a significant impact on Twist’s future GAAP financial results.

About Twist Bioscience

At Twist Bioscience, our customizable solutions across the biological continuum raise the bar in diagnostics, therapeutics, industrial, agriculture and research markets.

We drive innovation with confidence, without compromise. Whether delivering oligos, genes, proteins, libraries, characterization data, antibody discovery solutions, or NGS workflow tools, our scientific expertise and exceptional customer experience help navigate complex challenges, all with precision and at the scale and speed customers require. By enhancing R&D efficiency at every turn, we give scientists more shots on goal – more experiments, more iterations, more chances for remarkable discoveries.

Together, we stand with customers in the relentless pursuit of progress, backed by enterprise reliability, to shape a healthier and more sustainable future for all. For more information about our products and services, please visit www.twistbioscience.com.
