Consolidated operating results improved in the third quarter, driven by increased package base yields, higher package volumes, and continued structural cost reductions from business optimization initiatives. These factors were partially offset by increased purchased transportation rates, higher wage rates and variable compensation expenses, and the financial impact of global trade policy changes.

“As our strong operating results and adjusted free cash flow demonstrate, FedEx is winning across high-value market segments while we optimize our network, driving an increase to our full-year outlook,” said Raj Subramaniam, FedEx Corp. president and chief executive officer. “As we advance our transformation, we

1

remain committed to disciplined operational execution to reduce our cost-to-serve while focusing on profitable growth. We have the right strategy in place to continue to be the world’s premier B2B parcel and air freight provider, leverage our digital insights to unlock new markets, and use disciplined capital allocation to create additional stockholder value.”

Express U.S. Domestic segment operating income increased during the quarter, driven by higher package yields, increased package volume, improved service mix, and continued cost savings from transformation initiatives. These factors were partially offset by increased purchased transportation rates, and higher salaries and benefits expenses.

Express International segment operating results increased during the quarter, primarily due to increased base yields, higher international export volume, and increased freight weight. These factors were partially offset by higher wage rates, increased purchased transportation rates, and the financial impact of global trade policy changes.

FedEx Freight Spin-Off

FedEx completed the spin-off of FedEx Freight on June 1, 2026, retaining a 19.9% ownership interest in FedEx Freight. FedEx recognized a loss of $1.2 billion during the quarter within “Gain/(loss) on short-term investment” on the income statement. The carrying value of this equity security totaled $3.3 billion at September 30, 2026 and is recorded within “Short-term investment” in the condensed consolidated balance sheets.

FedEx utilized cash proceeds from the approximately $4.1 billion dividend received from FedEx Freight following the spin-off, as well as cash on hand, to fund cash tender offers for approximately $4.9 billion aggregate principal amount of outstanding debt securities, resulting in a non-GAAP, pre-tax gain of $627 million.

FedEx Supply Chain Sale

On October 1, 2026, FedEx completed the sale of its subsidiary FedEx Supply Chain to CMA CGM Group for a purchase price of approximately $1.4 billion. The transaction represents another important step in the ongoing transformation of FedEx, advancing its strategy to simplify its portfolio, sharpen its focus, and concentrate resources on the areas where the company is best positioned to create long-term value.

2027 Rate Increases

As previously announced, effective January 4, 2027, FedEx parcel shipping rates will increase by an average of 5.9%. Details related to these and additional changes to rates and surcharges are available at fedex.com/en-us/shipping/rate-changes.html.

Cash and Capital Returns

Cash on-hand as of September 30, 2026 was $8 billion, which included approximately $2.7 billion in IEEPA tariff refunds held for refund to customers.

FedEx completed $2.0 billion in stock repurchases via open market and accelerated share repurchase transactions in June through September. Approximately 6.3 million shares were repurchased, with the decrease in outstanding shares benefiting third quarter results by $0.02 per diluted share. FedEx will continue to be opportunistic with stock repurchases. As of September 30, 2026, $4.3 billion remained available for repurchases under the company’s 2026 stock repurchase authorization.

“Our solid third quarter results and higher 2026 financial outlook reflect the focused execution of our strategy, demonstrating momentum toward our 2029 financial targets,” said Claude Russ, FedEx Corp. enterprise vice president and interim chief financial officer. “Our decision to repurchase $2 billion of FedEx stock displays our conviction in achieving our 2029 financial targets and our commitment to deploy our strong free cash flow to increase stockholder returns.”

RECONCILIATIONS OF NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES
TO GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

Results for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025

The company reports its financial results in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP” or “reported”). We have supplemented the reporting of our financial information determined in accordance with GAAP with certain non-GAAP (or “adjusted”) financial measures, including our adjusted consolidated operating income and margin, income taxes, net income and diluted earnings per share, adjusted Express U.S. Domestic segment operating income and margin, and adjusted Express International segment operating income and margin for the 3-months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025. These financial measures are based on continuing operations and have been adjusted to exclude the effects of the following items (as applicable):
•Gain/Loss on retained investment in FedEx Freight: On June 1, 2026, FedEx completed the spin-off of FedEx Freight into an independent, publicly traded company (the “Spin-Off”). Following the Spin-Off, we retained a 19.9% ownership interest in FedEx Freight, which is accounted for as an equity security and recorded at fair value. Changes in fair value are recognized through net income.
•Gain on extinguishment of debt: On July 14, 2026, FedEx utilized the cash proceeds from the dividend received from FedEx Freight in connection with the Spin-Off, as well as cash on hand, to complete cash tender offers for certain outstanding debt securities. The gain on partial extinguishment is net of a proportional amount of the related unamortized discount, premium and debt issuance costs attributable to the repurchased securities.
•Restructuring, exit and other costs incurred: These costs relate to the multi-year restructuring, business realignment, and exit activities designed to improve operating efficiency, reduce costs, or optimize FedEx’s business portfolio. These costs also include those related to the Spin-Off and FedEx’s change in fiscal year end from May 31 to December 31. These costs were primarily related to severance, incentive payments to contracted service providers, and professional services.
◦Deferred income tax benefit on FedEx Supply Chain basis difference: A $263 million deferred income tax benefit has been recorded for the difference in the U.S. income tax and US GAAP book basis with respect to the FedEx Supply Chain business that was classified to held for sale in the third quarter.

The income tax effect of these costs is calculated based upon the tax laws and statutory income tax rates applicable in the tax jurisdiction(s) of the underlying non-GAAP adjustment. The impact of these non-GAAP items on the company's
