BLOOMIA HOLDINGS, INC. ANNOUNCES SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS

​

MINNEAPOLIS, MN – November 12, 2026 – Bloomia Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: TULP) (“Bloomia Holdings” or the “Company”) today announced its financial results for the first fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026.

​

The Company reported revenues of $5.0 million for the quarter, compared to revenues of $5.2 million in the comparable prior year quarter. Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter was a loss of $1.9 million compared to a loss of $2.4 million in the comparable prior year quarter. The Company reported a net loss of $2.4 million compared to a net loss of $2.9 million in the comparable prior year quarter.

​

Bloomia Holding’s Chairman and Co-Chief Executive Officer, Mark Jundt, commented, “Our revenue remained stable during the quarter, while we continued to make meaningful progress on the key operational and financial initiatives that are strengthening Bloomia's foundation for long-term growth. This progress is evident in our quarter’s results, where we improved our net loss by 17% and our adjusted EBITDA by 21% over the comparable prior year quarter. Further, we used significantly less cash in the quarter as Dutch bulb prices dropped materially year-over-year. This both strengthens our balance sheet by preserving cash, and leads to improved operating margins for the year ahead."

​

Co-Chief Executive Officer Dan Philp added, “Our first fiscal quarter is where we lay the foundation for a successful year. We have dug deep into analyzing our core operating processes, resulting in many process improvements that are simultaneously increasing quality and decreasing waste. We are set up for a year of growth in terms of both revenue and profitability.”              

​

Overview

​

Three Months Ended September 30, 2026

●Net revenue was $5.0 million compared to $5.2 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025.
●Gross loss was $0.2 million, or (4.5)% of sales, compared to $0.06 million, or (1.2)% of sales, in the three months ended September 30, 2025.
●Operating loss of $3.1 million, compared to an operating loss of $3.0 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025.
●Net loss was $2.9 million compared to $3.4 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025.
●Net loss attributable to Bloomia Holdings was $2.4 million, or a loss of $0.50 per diluted share, compared to net loss of $2.9 million, or a loss of $1.61 per diluted share, in the three months ended September 30, 2025.
●Adjusted EBITDA was a loss of $1.9 million compared to adjusted EBITDA loss of $2.4 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025.
●Cash used in operations was $4.7 million compared to $9.9 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025.

​

Q1 Fiscal Year 2027 Results

Net Revenue

Net revenue was $5.0 million in the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $5.2 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease is primarily due to lower stem sales due to excess waste, partially offset by an increase in prices.

Gross loss

Gross loss in the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $0.2 million, or (4.5)% of sales, compared to gross loss of $0.06 million, or (1.2)% of sales, in the three months ended September 30, 2025. The Company sold approximately 11% less stems in the three months ended September 30, 2026 resulting in a decrease in margin. Additionally, the Company recorded a one-time non-cash write-off of $0.4 million related to ex-force bulbs that were harvested for less value than expected. Excluding the one-time non-cash write-off, the Company’s gross profit was $0.2 million.

Operating loss

The Company had an operating loss of $3.1 million in the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to operating loss of $3.0 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in operating loss is primarily due to the increase in gross loss caused by the one-time non-cash write-off. Selling, general and administrative costs declined year over year.

Net loss

Net loss was $2.9 million in the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $3.4 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025. The reduction in the net loss is primarily due to a lower foreign currency transaction loss, a reduction in interest expense, and an increase in the income tax benefit.

Net loss attributable to Bloomia Holdings

Net loss attributable to Bloomia Holdings for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $2.4 million, or $0.50 per diluted share, compared to net loss attributable to Bloomia Holdings of $2.8 million, or $1.61 per diluted share, in the three months ended September 30, 2025. The reduction in the net loss is due to a lower foreign currency transaction loss, a reduction in interest expense, and an increase in the income tax benefit.

Adjusted EBITDA

In the three months ended September 30, 2026, adjusted EBITDA was a loss of $1.9 million, compared to an adjusted EBITDA loss of $2.4 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025. Adjusted EBITDA excludes the $0.4 million non-cash ex-force bulbs write-off in the period. Adjusted EBITDA improved due to higher prices, lower general and administrative costs and a lower foreign currency transaction loss.

Balance Sheet

As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents totaled $0.7 million, compared to $1.5 million as of June 30, 2025. Working capital (current assets less current liabilities) was $6.5 million at September 30, 2026, compared to $7.3 million at June 30, 2026. Working capital historically peaks in December and gradually reaches the trough around June 30 due to the seasonality of the business. The Company had $8.6 million drawn on its revolver as of September 30, 2026, compared to $4.0 million drawn on its revolver as of June 30, 2026. As of September 30, 2026, the Company classified $2.6 million of the revolver balance as a current liability, which decreases working capital.

About Bloomia Holdings, Inc.

On January 28, 2026, the Company changed its name to Bloomia Holdings, Inc. and began trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the name Bloomia Holdings, Inc. and under new ticker symbol “TULP”. The CUSIP of the Common Stock did not change in connection with the name change or the ticker symbol change.

Bloomia Holdings, Inc (Nasdaq: TULP) is a specialty ag company focused on making and managing its ag investments in the U.S. and internationally. The Company is the majority owner of Bloomia, one of the largest producers of fresh-cut tulips in the United States.
