L.B. Foster Announces Third Quarter Results Reflecting Strong Sales and Profitability Growth; Finishes Quarter with Lowest Debt Level in a Decade

Unless otherwise noted, all comparisons are to the third quarter of 2025.

•Third quarter sales increased 21.5% to $168.0 million; gross profit up $3.4 million or 10.8%
•Third quarter net income grew 56.5% to $6.8 million; Adjusted EBITDA1 up 15.1% to $13.1 million
•First nine months sales increased 12.7% to $427.7 million; gross profit up $8.9 million or 10.8%
•First nine months net income grew 125.4% to $11.4 million; Adjusted EBITDA up 17.6% to $29.9 million
•Generated quarterly operating cash flow of $23.6 million with total debt for the quarter at $28.7 million
•Gross Leverage Ratio1 improved 1.0x turns year over year, ending the quarter at 0.6x
PITTSBURGH, PA, November 4, 2026 – L.B. Foster Company (Nasdaq: FSTR), a global technology solutions provider of products and services for the rail and infrastructure markets (the "Company"), today reported its 2026 third quarter operating results.

John Kasel, President and Chief Executive Officer, commented, "We started the second half of the year with strong momentum, delivering meaningful growth in sales, profitability, and cash generation during the third quarter. Sales increased 21.5% over the prior year quarter, reflecting strong growth across both segments, while Adjusted EBITDA1 increased 15.1%. Gross profit expanded $3.4 million during the quarter despite a decline in gross margin of 200 bps

•Sales for the 2026 third quarter increased $29.7 million, or 21.5%, over the prior year quarter, driven by higher sales in both segments. Rail increased $25.1 million, or 32.3%, while Infrastructure increased $4.6 million, or 7.6%.

•Gross profit for the 2026 third quarter improved by $3.4 million or 10.8% over the prior year quarter. Rail gross profit increased $2.4 million and Infrastructure gross profit increased $0.9 million driven by improved volumes in both segments. Gross margins declined 200 basis points to 20.5% driven by business mix in Rail due to sales growth in our lower-margin Rail Distribution and UK businesses.

•Selling and administrative expenses for the 2026 third quarter increased $1.7 million, or 7.5%, over the prior year quarter, primarily due to increased employment costs, including higher variable incentive-based compensation costs and medical costs. Selling and administrative expenses as a percentage of net sales decreased 190 basis points to 14.1%.



•Operating income for the 2026 third quarter increased $1.8 million, or 21.5%, over the prior year quarter. The increase was driven by improved gross profit offset in part by higher selling and administrative costs.

•Net income attributable to the Company for the 2026 third quarter improved $2.4 million, or 55.4%, over the prior year quarter due to improved operating income, reduced interest expense, and a lower income tax expense.

•Adjusted EBITDA for the 2026 third quarter increased $1.7 million, or 15.1%, over the prior year quarter.

•Cash provided by operating activities totaled $23.6 million in the 2026 third quarter, unfavorable $5.6 million compared to $29.2 million in the prior year quarter.

•Total debt as of September 30, 2026 was $28.7 million, decreasing by $30.0 million from the prior year quarter and by $19.3 million during the quarter. The reduction is due to strong Free Cash Flow generation and lower working capital requirements during the period. The Company's Gross Leverage Ratio per its credit facility was 0.6x as of September 30, 2026, down from 1.6x last year, reflecting higher profitability and lower working capital.

•New orders, net for the 2026 third quarter increased $41.0 million, or 35.7%, over the prior year quarter, reflecting growth in Infrastructure and due to a $19 million order that was cancelled in the prior year. The trailing twelve month book-to-bill ratio1 was 0.98 : 1.00. Backlog decreased $12.7 million, or 5.1%, from the prior year quarter, as a 9.8% decline in Rail backlog more than offset a modest 1.0% increase in Infrastructure backlog. Sequentially, backlog decreased $11.4 million, or 4.6%, primarily due to higher sales volumes and seasonally lower order activity.

•Sales for the first nine months of 2026 increased $48.1 million, or 12.7%, over the prior year period. The increase was driven by Rail sales growth of $41.9 million, or 20.2%, led by Rail Products which increased $23.3 million, or 19.2%. Global Friction Management and TS&S also increased $7.9 million, or 13.8%, and $10.7 million, or 36.9%, respectively. Infrastructure sales improved $6.1 million, or 3.6%. The improvement was driven primarily by Precast with sales growth of $9.7 million, or 8.2%, offset in part by Steel Products which declined $3.5 million, or 6.5%.

•Gross profit for the first nine months of 2026 increased $8.9 million, or 10.8%, over the prior year period driven by improved volumes in Rail which increased $6.2 million. Rail gross profit for the first nine months of 2026 includes $2.1 million of costs associated with the exit of certain product lines within the Tew Engineering business (the "Tew Exit") and for the first nine months of 2025 includes $1.1 million of costs associated with the exit of the UK Automation and Materials Handling product line (the "AMH Exit"). Infrastructure gross profit improved $2.7 million due to favorable business mix and manufacturing efficiency. Gross margins declined by 30 basis points to 21.3% due to sales growth in our lower-margin Rail Distribution and UK businesses.

•Selling and administrative expenses for the first nine months of 2026 increased $5.5 million, or 8.3%, over the prior year period, primarily due to higher employment costs including higher variable incentive-based compensation costs and medical costs. Selling and administrative expenses for the first nine months of 2026 included $0.6 million of Tew Exit and other strategic initiatives costs including the Dulworth acquisition compared to the first nine months of 2025 which included $0.3 million of AMH Exit costs. Selling and administrative expenses as a percentage of net sales decreased 60 basis points to 16.6%.

•Operating income for the first nine months of 2026 improved $4.2 million over the prior year period. The improvement was primarily driven by higher gross profit and lower amortization expense partially offset by an increase in selling and administrative expenses.

•Net income attributable to the Company for the first nine months of 2026 improved $6.2 million over the prior year period driven by higher operating income, reduced interest expense, and lower income tax expense.

•Adjusted EBITDA for the first nine months of 2026 improved $4.5 million, or 17.6%, over the prior year period and was adjusted for $0.4 million of strategic initiative costs including the Dulworth acquisition and $2.3 million of Tew Exit costs. The first nine months of 2025 was adjusted for the $1.4 million of AMH Exit costs.

•Cash provided by operating activities totaled $31.0 million for the first nine months of 2026, a $17.5 million improvement compared to cash provided by operating activities of $13.4 million in the prior year period.



•New orders, net for the first nine months of 2026 increased $34.3 million, or 7.8%, over the prior year period, driven by a 24.6% increase in Infrastructure, which benefited from an order cancellation in the Steel Products business during the prior year period, and more than offset a 1.4% decline in Rail.

About L.B. Foster Company
Founded in 1902, L.B. Foster Company is a global technology solutions provider of products and services for the rail and infrastructure markets. The Company’s innovative engineering and product development solutions address the safety, reliability, and performance needs of its customers' most challenging requirements. The Company maintains locations in North America, South America, Europe, and Asia. For more information, please visit www.lbfoster.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This press release contains financial measures that are not calculated and presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States ("GAAP"). These non-GAAP financial measures are provided as additional information for investors. The presentation of this additional information is not meant to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for GAAP measures. For definitions of the non-GAAP financial measures used in this press release and reconciliations to the most directly comparable respective GAAP measures, see the “Non-GAAP Disclosures” section below.

The Company has not reconciled the forward-looking Adjusted EBITDA and Free Cash Flow to the most directly comparable GAAP measure because this cannot be done without unreasonable effort due to the variability and low visibility with respect to certain costs, the most significant of which are acquisition and divestiture-related costs, impairment expense, and changes in operating assets and liabilities. These underlying expenses and others that may arise during the year are potential adjustments to future earnings. The Company expects the variability of these items to have a potentially unpredictable, and a potentially significant, impact on our future GAAP financial results.

The Company believes Free Cash Flow is useful information to investors as it provides insight on cash generated by operations, less capital expenditures, which we believe to be helpful in assessing the Company's long-term ability to pursue growth and investment opportunities as well as service its financing obligations and generate capital for shareholders. Additionally, the Company's annual incentive plans for management provide for the utilization of Free Cash Flow as a metric for measuring cash-generation performance in determining annual variable incentive achievement.

The Company defines new orders, net as a contractual agreement between the Company and a third-party in which the Company will, or has the ability to, satisfy the performance obligations of the promised products or services under the terms of the agreement net of order cancellations incurred during the period. The Company defines backlog as contractual commitments to customers for which the Company’s performance obligations have not been met, including with respect to new orders and contracts for which the Company has not begun any performance. Backlog may not be indicative of future operating results as orders may be cancelled or modified by the customer. Management utilizes new orders, net and backlog to evaluate the health of the industries in which the Company operates, the Company’s current and future results of operations and financial prospects, and strategies for business development. The Company believes that new orders, net and backlog are useful to investors as supplemental metrics by which to measure the Company’s current performance and prospective results of operations and financial performance. The Company defines book-to-bill ratio as new orders, net divided by revenue. The Company believes this is a useful metric to assess supply and demand, including order strength versus order fulfillment.



The Company views its Gross Leverage Ratio per its credit agreement, as defined in the Fifth Amended and Restated Credit Agreement dated June 27, 2025, as an important indication of the Company's financial health and believes it is useful to investors as an indicator of the Company's ability to service its existing indebtedness and borrow additional funds for its operational and investing needs, including capital expenditures and acquisitions.

This earnings release discloses earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (“EBITDA”), Adjusted EBITDA, and Free Cash Flow. The Company believes that EBITDA is useful to investors as a supplemental way to evaluate the ongoing operations of the Company’s business since EBITDA may enhance investors’ ability to compare historical periods as it adjusts for the impact of financing methods, tax law and strategy changes, and depreciation and amortization. In addition, EBITDA is a financial measure that management and the Company’s Board of Directors use in their financial and operational decision-making and in the determination of certain compensation programs. Adjusted EBITDA adjusts for certain charges to EBITDA from continuing operations that the Company believes are unusual, non-recurring, unpredictable, or non-cash.

In the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company made adjustments to exclude strategic initiative costs, including acquisition-related costs, and in the three months ended September 30, 2025, the Company made no such adjustments to EBITDA. In the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company made adjustments to exclude costs associated with the Tew Exit and strategic initiative costs including acquisition-related costs. In the nine months ended September 30, 2025, the Company made adjustments to exclude AMH Exit costs. The Company believes the results adjusted to exclude these items are useful to investors as these items are non-routine in nature.

Non-GAAP financial measures are not a substitute for GAAP financial results and should only be considered in conjunction with the Company’s financial information that is presented in accordance with GAAP.
