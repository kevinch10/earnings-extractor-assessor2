BLACKROCK TCP CAPITAL CORP. ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS INCLUDING NET INVESTMENT INCOME OF $0.16 PER SHARE; DECLARES A FOURTH QUARTER DIVIDEND OF $0.17 PER SHARE

 

SANTA MONICA, Calif., November 5, 2026 - BlackRock TCP Capital Corp. (“we,” “us,” “our,” “TCPC” or the “Company”), a business development company (NASDAQ: TCPC), today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and filed its Form 10-Q with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

 

FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS

•
On a GAAP basis, net investment income for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $13.4 million, or $0.16 per share on a diluted basis, which did not exceed the regular dividend of $0.17 per share paid on September 30, 2026. Adjusted net investment income(1) for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $12.5 million, or $0.15 per share on a diluted basis.
•
Net decrease in net assets from operations on a GAAP basis for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $66.8 million, or $0.80 per share, compared to a $1.7 million, or $0.02 per share, net increase in net assets from operations for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.
•
Net asset value per share was $5.61 as of September 30, 2026, compared to $6.58 as of June 30, 2026.
•
As of September 30, 2026, investments on non-accrual status represented 3.4% of the portfolio at fair value and 14.2% at cost, compared to 1.6% of the portfolio at fair value and 7.4% at cost as of June 30, 2026.
•
Net leverage was 0.16x as of September 30, 2026, compared to 1.38x as of June 30, 2026.
•
As previously announced, on August 4, 2026, the Company completed the sale of 95% of the equity interests in a vehicle (the "Continuation Vehicle") holding approximately $524 million of investments across 78 portfolio companies to funds and accounts sponsored by Pantheon, a global leader in private credit secondaries.
•
On November 5, 2026, our Board of Directors (the "Board") declared a fourth quarter dividend of $0.17 per share, payable on December 31, 2026 to stockholders of record as of the close of business on December 17, 2026.

 

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY

“Strong repayment and realization activity this quarter, together with proceeds from the portfolio sale, significantly reduced leverage and meaningfully enhanced liquidity in the portfolio,” said Jason Mehring, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of BlackRock TCP Capital Corp. “Together, these steps place TCPC on a strong foundation as the Board conducts its ongoing strategic review, which is focused on achieving the best possible outcome for shareholders.”

(1) Excluding amortization of purchase discount recorded in connection with the Merger (defined herein), adjusted net investment income for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $12.5 million, or $0.15 per share on a diluted basis.

On March 18, 2024, the Company completed its previously announced merger (the "Merger") with BlackRock Capital Investment Corporation ("BCIC"). The Merger has been accounted for as an asset acquisition of BCIC by the Company in accordance with the asset acquisition method of accounting as detailed in ASC 805-50 ("ASC 805"), Business Combinations-Related Issues. The Company determined the fair value of the shares of the Company's common stock that were issued to former BCIC shareholders pursuant to the Merger Agreement plus transaction costs to be the consideration paid in connection with the Merger under ASC 805. The consideration paid to BCIC shareholders was less than the aggregate fair values of the BCIC assets acquired and liabilities assumed, which resulted in a purchase discount (the “purchase discount”). The consideration paid was allocated to the individual BCIC assets acquired and liabilities assumed based on the relative fair values of net identifiable assets acquired other than “non-qualifying” assets and liabilities (for example, cash) and did not give rise to goodwill. As a result, the purchase discount was allocated to the cost basis of the BCIC investments acquired by the Company on a pro-rata basis based on their relative fair values as of the effective time of the Merger. Immediately following the Merger, the investments were marked to their respective fair values in accordance with ASC 820 which resulted in immediate recognition of net unrealized appreciation in the Consolidated Statement of Operations as a result of the Merger. The purchase discount allocated to the BCIC debt investments acquired will amortize over the remaining life of each respective debt investment through interest income, with a corresponding adjustment recorded to unrealized appreciation or depreciation on such investment acquired through its ultimate disposition. The purchase discount allocated to BCIC equity investments acquired will not amortize over the life of such investments through interest income and, assuming no subsequent change to the fair value of the equity investments acquired and disposition of such equity investments at fair value, the Company may recognize a realized gain or loss with a corresponding reversal of the unrealized appreciation on disposition of such equity investments acquired.

As a supplement to the Company’s reported GAAP financial measures, we have provided the following non-GAAP financial measures that we believe are useful:

•
“Adjusted net investment income” – excludes the amortization of purchase accounting discount from net investment income calculated in accordance with GAAP;

 

 

•
“Adjusted net realized and unrealized gain (loss)” – excludes the unrealized appreciation resulting from the purchase discount and the corresponding reversal of the unrealized appreciation from the amortization of the purchase discount from the determination of net realized and unrealized gain (loss) determined in accordance with GAAP; and
•
“Adjusted net increase (decrease) in net assets resulting from operations” – calculates net increase (decrease) in net assets resulting from operations based on Adjusted net investment income and Adjusted net realized and unrealized gain (loss).

 

We believe that the adjustment to exclude the full effect of purchase discount accounting under ASC 805 from these financial measures is meaningful because of the potential impact on the comparability of these financial measures that we and investors use to assess our financial condition and results of operations period over period. Although these non-GAAP financial measures are intended to enhance investors’ understanding of our business and performance, these non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered an alternative to GAAP. The aforementioned non-GAAP financial measures may not be comparable to similar non-GAAP financial measures used by other companies.

 

 

 

PORTFOLIO AND INVESTMENT ACTIVITY

As of September 30, 2026, our consolidated investment portfolio consisted of debt and equity positions in 126 portfolio companies with a total fair value of approximately $543.5 million, of which 79.6% was in senior secured debt. 79.6% of the total portfolio was first lien. Equity positions represented approximately 20.4% of the portfolio. 88.0% of our debt investments were floating rate, 89.0% of which had interest rate floors.

As of September 30, 2026, the weighted average annual effective yield of our debt portfolio was approximately 11.1%(1) and the weighted average annual effective yield of our total portfolio was approximately 9.4%, compared to 11.2% and 10.5%, respectively, as of June 30, 2026. Investments in twelve portfolio companies were on non-accrual status as of September 30, 2026, representing 3.4% of the consolidated portfolio at fair value and 14.2% at cost, compared to 1.6% of the portfolio at fair value and 7.4% at cost as of June 30, 2026.

During the three months ended September 30, 2026, we invested approximately $6.2 million across existing portfolio companies. Of these investments, $5.7 million, or 91.5%, were in first lien senior secured loans. The remaining $0.5 million, or 8.5%, were comprised of equity investments. Additionally, sales and repayments of investments totaled approximately $681.4 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026, including $457.5 million of investments, net of losses, sold through the Continuation Vehicle transaction. New investments during the quarter had a weighted average effective yield of 11.1%. Investments we exited had a weighted average effective yield of 10.9%.

As of September 30, 2026, total assets were $816.5 million, net assets were $471.0 million and net asset value per share was $5.61, as compared to $1.5 billion, $552.0 million, and $6.58 per share, respectively, as of June 30, 2026.

CONSOLIDATED RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Total investment income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $28.5 million, or $0.34 per share. Investment income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 included recurring cash interest income of $0.20 per share, $0.08 per share from prepayment premiums and related accelerated original issue discount and exit fee amortization, $0.02 per share from recurring portfolio investment original issue discount and exit fee amortization, $0.03 per share from interest income paid in kind and $0.01 per share in dividend income. This reflects our policy of recording interest income, adjusted for amortization of portfolio investment premiums and discounts, on an accrual basis. Origination, structuring, closing, commitment, and similar upfront fees received in connection with the outlay of capital are generally amortized into interest income over the life of the respective debt investment.

Total operating expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were approximately $15.1 million, or $0.18 per share, including interest and other debt expenses of $9.4 million, or $0.11 per share and base management fees of $3.0 million, or $0.04 per share. As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s cumulative total return did not exceed the total return hurdle, and as a result, no incentive compensation was accrued for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Excluding interest and other debt expenses, annualized third quarter expenses were 4.4% of average net assets.

Net investment income for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $13.4 million, or $0.16 per share. Net realized loss on investments and foreign currency for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $74.5 million, or $0.89 per share. Net realized loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was comprised primarily of $63.7 million in losses from the Continuation Vehicle Transaction, and $4.2 million and $3.8 million in losses from the disposition of our investments in Paramount and Emerald, respectively, offset by $4.7 million in gains from the disposition of our investments in Domo. Net unrealized loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $4.3 million, or $0.05 per share. Net unrealized loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 primarily reflects an $11.5 million unrealized loss on our investment in Job and Talent, a $6.9 million reversal of previously recognized unrealized gains from the disposition of our investment in Domo, and a $2.5 million reversal of previously recognized unrealized gains from the disposition of our investment in Thras.io, partially offset by an $8.3 million reversal of previously recognized unrealized losses from the disposition of our investments in the Continuation Vehicle Transaction, and $5.7 million and $4.7 million reversals of previously recognized unrealized losses from the disposition of our investments in Paramount and Emerald, respectively. Realized loss on extinguishment of debt was $1.3 million, or $0.02 per share, for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Net decrease in net assets resulting from operations for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $66.8 million, or $0.80 per share.

ABOUT BLACKROCK TCP CAPITAL CORP.

BlackRock TCP Capital Corp. (NASDAQ: TCPC) is a specialty finance company focused on direct lending to middle-market companies as well as small businesses. TCPC lends primarily to companies with established market positions, strong regional or national operations, differentiated products and services and sustainable competitive advantages, investing across industries in which it has significant knowledge and expertise. TCPC’s investment objective is to achieve high total returns through current income and capital appreciation, with an emphasis on principal protection. TCPC is a publicly-traded business development company, or BDC, regulated under the Investment Company Act of 1940 and is externally managed by its advisor, an indirect subsidiary of BlackRock, Inc. For more information, visit www.tcpcapital.com.
