Co-Diagnostics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Two Regulatory Submissions Under Review in India and the U.S.

 

Third Quarter Operating Expenses Down 12% Year Over Year

 

Platform Capabilities Expanding with ReadyGo Collaboration, India Connectivity and Vector Smart Growth

 

SALT LAKE CITY, November 10, 2026 – Co-Diagnostics, Inc. (Nasdaq: CODX) (“Co-Dx” or “the Company”), a molecular diagnostics company with a unique, patented platform for the development of molecular diagnostic tests, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Business Highlights:

 

●Submitted a 510(k) premarket notification and concurrent CLIA Waiver by Application in August to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for the Co-Dx PCR Flu A/B & RSV test on the Co-Dx PCR Pro instrument, supported by a clinical study of more than 1,400 symptomatic patients at nine U.S. clinical sites

 

●Entered a collaboration with ReadyGo Diagnostics to evaluate blood-based molecular testing at the point of need, with an initial focus on decentralized Ebola detection

 

●Completed a proof-of-concept study in July in which an extraction-free Co-Primers Ebola assay using diluted plasma ran on the Co-Dx PCR Pro instrument, the Company’s first plasma-based assay designed for the instrument

 

●Launched the Company’s integrated cloud and mobile infrastructure in India, which supports tuberculosis (TB) clinical work and is designed to analyze results on a mobile device without an internet connection and synchronize data to the cloud when connectivity is available

 

●Shipped Vector Smart® laboratory equipment, consumables and reagents to an overseas U.S. Air Force base, marking the first international deployment of the platform

 

●Completed two new Vector Smart® laboratory installations in Florida, with installation and customer training underway at an additional laboratory in Indiana, expanding a network that serves customers across 21 U.S. states

 

●Demonstrated automation of the Company’s test-cup manufacturing line at a September press conference, reducing cycle time by approximately 45% and increasing output by approximately 74% compared with the manual process

 

●Advanced CoSara’s Bundibugyo virus and pan-Ebola tests for centralized laboratories and responded to a request from India’s Centre for Cellular and Molecular Platforms for third-party evaluation of the Bundibugyo test

 

●Participated in global health forums, including the UNOPS near point-of-care TB workshop in Bangkok, the joint WHO, Unitaid and UNAIDS Annual Consultation in Geneva, and events during UN General Assembly week

 

●Appointed Wes Lindsey, Ph.D., MBA, as Chief Scientific Officer; Dr. Lindsey brings more than 20 years of experience in molecular diagnostics and is leading the Company’s work with the FDA through its review

 

 
 

 

“The third quarter was a period of significant regulatory and operational progress for Co-Diagnostics,” said Dwight Egan, Chief Executive Officer of Co-Diagnostics. “CoSara completed its clinical performance study for our point-of-care tuberculosis test and submitted it to India’s Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO), and together with our August FDA 510(k) submission for the Flu A/B & RSV test, we now have two regulatory submissions under review in two important markets for our platform. We also launched our cloud and mobile infrastructure in India, began evaluating blood-based testing with ReadyGo, and completed the first international deployment of Vector Smart. With our submissions in India and the U.S. under review, our focus is on supporting those reviews, advancing our feasibility work with ReadyGo and continuing to grow Vector Smart.”

 

Recent Developments:

 

●Submitted the CoSara PCR MTB test, a point-of-care tuberculosis test, to the CDSCO through CoSara Diagnostics, following completion of the clinical performance study.

 

●Completed the CoSara PCR MTB clinical performance study of 676 clinical samples; in the 233 samples paired with MGIT testing, the test demonstrated average sensitivity of 97.3% and average specificity of 96.0% across three test batches.

 

●Secured an order for a full Vector Smart® mosquito surveillance laboratory installation from the Ute Indian Tribe Mosquito Abatement Department, which will enable the Tribe to perform mosquito pool PCR testing in house.

 

●Highlighted that sputum sample-preparation technology developed for the Company’s TB program could provide a foundation for plague diagnostics on the Co-Dx PCR Pro® platform, should a public health need arise.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results:

 

●Revenue of $0.3 million, compared to $0.1 million in the third quarter of 2025

 

●Operating expenses of $6.2 million, compared to $7.1 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to decreased personnel and stock-based compensation expense and lower Co-Dx PCR platform development expense, partially offset by higher legal and professional services expense

 

●Operating loss of $6.3 million, compared to $7.0 million in the third quarter of 2025

 

●Net loss of $6.3 million, or $1.01 per basic and diluted share, compared to a net loss of $5.9 million, or $4.66 per basic and diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year increase in net loss was primarily attributable to an income tax benefit recognized in the prior-year period, partially offset by reduced operating expenses

 

●Adjusted EBITDA loss of $5.9 million, compared to a loss of $6.3 million in the third quarter of 2025

 

●Cash and cash equivalents totaled $0.7 million as of September 30, 2026

 

●For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, operating expenses were $21.7 million and net cash used in operating activities was $20.7 million, compared to $23.9 million and $22.9 million, respectively, in the prior-year period

 

●

Subsequent to quarter end, the Company completed a senior secured convertible debt financing with J.J. Astor & Co., with approximately $2.1 million in initial funding before other transaction expenses, and with up to an additional $1.0 million available subject to specified conditions

About Co-Diagnostics, Inc.:

 

Co-Diagnostics, Inc., a Utah corporation, is a molecular diagnostics company that develops, manufactures and markets state-of-the-art diagnostics technologies. The Company’s technologies are utilized for tests that are designed using the detection and/or analysis of nucleic acid molecules (DNA or RNA). The Company also uses its proprietary technology to design specific tests for its Co-Dx PCR at-home and point-of-care platform (subject to regulatory review and not currently for sale) and to identify genetic markers for use in applications other than infectious disease.

 

Non-GAAP Financial Measures:

 

This press release contains adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure defined as net income (loss) adjusted for depreciation, amortization, income tax (benefit) expense, net interest (income) expense, stock-based compensation, change in fair value of contingent consideration, and realized gain (loss) on investments. The Company believes that adjusted EBITDA provides useful information to management and investors relating to its results of operations. The Company’s management uses this non-GAAP measure to compare the Company’s performance to that of prior periods for trend analyses, and for budgeting and planning purposes. The Company believes that the use of adjusted EBITDA provides an additional tool for investors to use in evaluating ongoing operating results and trends and in comparing the Company’s financial measures with other companies, many of which present similar non-GAAP financial measures to investors, and that it allows for greater transparency with respect to key metrics used by management in its financial and operational decision-making.

 

Management does not consider the non-GAAP measure in isolation or as an alternative to financial measures determined in accordance with GAAP. The principal limitation of the non-GAAP financial measure is that it excludes significant expenses that are required by GAAP to be recorded in the Company’s financial statements. In order to compensate for these limitations, management presents the non-GAAP financial measure together with GAAP results. Non-GAAP measures should be considered in addition to results prepared in accordance with GAAP, but should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, GAAP results. A reconciliation table of the net income, the most comparable GAAP financial measure to adjusted EBITDA, is included at the end of this release. The Company urges investors to review the reconciliation and not to rely on any single financial measure to evaluate the company’s business.
