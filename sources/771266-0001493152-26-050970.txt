Kopin Corporation Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Third Quarter Revenue Grows 52% Year-over-Year to $18.2 Million; Loss from Operations Narrows Sequentially to $0.5 Million
Additional $18.6 Million U.S. Army IBAS Award Brings Total IBAS Funding to $34 Million; Neural I/o™ on Track for First Public Demonstration at CES 2027
WESTBOROUGH, MA — November 10, 2026 — Kopin Corporation (Nasdaq: KOPN) (“Kopin” or the “Company”), a leading provider of application-specific optical systems and high-performance microdisplays for defense, AI-infrastructure, enterprise, industrial, consumer and medical products, today announced preliminary unaudited financial results for its third quarter ended September 26, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 & Subsequent Operational Highlights
● | Secured an additional $18.6 million sole-source award from the Office of the Secretary of War through the U.S. Army Contracting Command under the Industrial Base Analysis and Sustainment (IBAS) program — bringing cumulative IBAS funding for Kopin’s color MicroLED technology to $34 million.
● | Advanced Neural I/o™, the MicroLED-based optical interconnect co-developed with Fabric.AI, toward its first public demonstration at CES in January 2027, to be followed by a demonstration at SPIE Photonics West. The companies began initial transmission-link testing and published “The Data Center in a SerDes-Free World,” a white paper modeling as much as 1.6 times greater useful data throughput from the same number of GPUs.
● | Awarded an $800,000 grant through the Massachusetts Manufacturing Innovation Initiative (M2I2) to install advanced capital equipment for domestic manufacturing of MicroLED displays and Neural I/o™ optical interconnects at the Company’s Westborough facility, and named Manufacturer of the Year by the Massachusetts Legislative Manufacturing Caucus.
● | Received a $2.4 million order for OLED microdisplays from a new customer through Kopin Europe. The customer is a leading international thermal weapon sight manufacturer whose program has since entered low-rate initial production, with deliveries scheduled to begin in the fourth quarter of 2026.
● | Introduced an expanded first-person viewer (FPV) evaluation kit lineup at AUSA 2026 to accelerate adoption of Kopin’s FPV optical modules among new and existing drone partners, and received prototype headset orders from more than a dozen new Sentinel FPV™ customers for use in the Drone Dominance Program and within other Department of War and U.S. government law enforcement agencies.
Management Commentary
Michael Murray, Chief Executive Officer of Kopin, said: “Third quarter revenue grew 52%, driven by funded development programs that are setting the stage for future product revenue as they move from R&D into production. The additional $18.6 million IBAS award brings total funding for our color MicroLED technology to $34 million, keeping us on track for MicroLED product manufacturing in mid-2027, and Neural I/o™ remains on schedule for its first public demonstration at CES in January.”
Erich Manz, Chief Financial Officer of Kopin, added: “Our third quarter results reflect the scaling of our funded development programs and growing operating leverage. We continue to expect a strong second half and meaningful progress toward sustainable GAAP profitability.”
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Total revenues for the third quarter ended September 26, 2026 increased 52% to $18.2 million, compared to $12.0 million for the third quarter ended September 27, 2025, and rose 43% sequentially from $12.7 million in the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year increase primarily reflects research and development revenue from the Company’s joint development program with Fabric.AI and grant revenue recognized under its U.S. Government color MicroLED award.
Cost of product revenues for the third quarter of 2026 was $6.4 million, or 73% of net product revenues, compared to $8.4 million, or 79% of net product revenues, for the third quarter of 2025, and 86% of net product revenues in the second quarter of 2026.
Operating expenses, consisting of research and development (R&D) and selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses, were $12.3 million for the third quarter of 2026. Sequentially, operating expenses increased 28% while total revenues grew 43%, reducing operating expenses by 800 basis points to 68% of total revenues. Year-over-year, operating expenses increased from $4.1 million in the third quarter of 2025, a period that benefited from an approximately $3.3 million reduction in accrued legal fees related to the prior litigation matters and a $3.9 million increase in funded R&D costs, which support the Company’s development and grant revenue.
Loss from operations for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.5 million, compared to income from operations of $4.5 million for the third quarter of 2025, which included a $5.1 million benefit from the reduction of a litigation accrual and the reduction in accrued legal fees noted above. Sequentially, loss from operations saw a $3.0 million improvement.
Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $3.4 million, or $(0.02) per share, compared to net income of $4.1 million, or $0.02 per share, in the third quarter of 2025. The third quarter 2026 net loss includes approximately $2.7 million of net losses on investments, largely reflecting non-cash changes in the fair value of the Company’s strategic equity investments.
As of September 26, 2026, the Company had cash and cash equivalents of $25.8 million, up from $24.9 million as of June 27, 2026, and total cash, restricted cash and marketable securities of $51.3 million, inclusive of $25.5 million of restricted cash, of which $24.2 million collateralizes the supersedeas bond posted in connection with a litigation appeal. Cash provided by operating activities was approximately $2.4 million in the third quarter, reducing net cash used in operating activities for the first nine months of 2026 to $2.7 million. Capital expenditures were $1.4 million in the third quarter.

About Kopin Corporation
Kopin Corporation (Nasdaq: KOPN) is a leading developer and provider of innovative display and application-specific optical solutions for defense, AI infrastructure, enterprise, professional and consumer products. Kopin’s portfolio includes microdisplays, display modules, eyepiece and projection assemblies, and vehicle- and head-mounted display systems built on the Company’s liquid crystal, MicroLED and OLED display technologies, along with a range of optics and low-power custom silicon. Building on its patented bi-directional NeuralDisplay™ architecture, Kopin is also developing Neural I/o™ optical interconnects that use programmable MicroLED pixels as ultra-high-speed, low-power optical transceivers for AI data centers. For more information, please visit Kopin’s website at www.kopin.com.
