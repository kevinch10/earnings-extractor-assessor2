Citizens Community Bancorp, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Earnings of $0.56 Per Share;
Board Approves 14% Increase in Quarterly Dividend to $0.12 per Share
EAU CLAIRE, WI, October 26, 2026 - Citizens Community Bancorp, Inc. (the “Company”) (Nasdaq: CZWI), the parent company of CCFBank N.A. (the “Bank” or “CCFBank”), today reported earnings of $5.4 million and earnings per diluted share of $0.56 for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $1.1 million and earnings per diluted share of $0.11 for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, and $3.7 million and $0.37 earnings per diluted share for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, respectively. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported earnings of $10.3 million and earnings per diluted share of $1.06 compared to the prior year period of $10.1 million and earnings per diluted share of $1.02.
Third quarter 2026 operating results reflected the following changes from the second quarter of 2026: (1) an increase in net interest income of $1.0 million largely due to a higher net interest margin of 15 basis points due to the impact of new loan originations and loans repricing; (2) a decrease in provision for credit losses of $4.2 million from $4.3 million the prior quarter; and (3) slightly higher non-interest income of $0.3 million largely due to higher loan servicing income and higher gains on equity securities.
Book value per share increased to $19.91 on September 30, 2026, compared to $19.83 at June 30, 2026, and increased from $18.95 at September 30, 2025. Tangible book value per share (non-GAAP)1 was $16.62 at September 30, 2026, compared to $16.55 at June 30, 2026, and $15.71 at September 30, 2025. For the third quarter of 2026, the increase in tangible book value was due to quarterly net income, partially offset by both the impact of higher unrealized losses on the available-for-sale investment portfolio, and the quarterly dividend payment. Stockholders’ equity as a percentage of total assets was 10.62% at September 30, 2026, compared to 10.55% at June 30, 2026. Tangible common equity (“TCE”) as a percent of tangible assets (non-GAAP)1 was 9.02% at September 30, 2026, compared to 8.96% at June 30, 2026.
“The strong quarter reflected continued execution of our strategic plan with lower provision expense and the net interest margin and margin dollar increase being the highlight. We also saw loan originations outpace a modest pickup in loan payoffs, ten percent higher non-interest income from the linked quarter and non-interest expenses were controlled other than higher variable performance-based compensation expense which accounted for the uptick from last quarter. Efforts to work through asset quality issues continued in the quarter. Net charge offs were $5 thousand and criticized loans increased at a slower rate from last quarter to $61.2 million. The number of relationships risk rated special mention or substandard is concentrated in ten relationships, which make up 70% of the above two risk ratings dollars. We believe these problem loan relationships represent individual company/management issues rather than a concentration or industry weakness.
Executive management has directed workout strategies for criticized loan relationships and led portfolio reviews during the quarter of select loan segments to enhance resolution efforts and align resources. We continue to monitor performance as updated borrower financial information is received.
Growth in capital through earnings absorbed unrealized losses in our securities portfolio and nudged our tangible capital ratio to 9.02%. Seventy thousand shares were repurchased in the quarter under the authorization announced in early September. On October 22, 2026, the board authorized an increase of the quarterly dividend by 14% to $0.12 per

share. The board and management continue to work with our investment bankers on strategic opportunities in the best interest of our shareholders,” said Stephen Bianchi, Chairman, President, and CEO.
September 30, 2026, Highlights:
•Quarterly earnings were $5.4 million, or $0.56 per diluted share for the quarter ending September 30, 2026, an increase compared to earnings of $1.1 million, or $0.11 per diluted share for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and an increase from $3.7 million, or $0.37 per diluted share for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
•For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, earnings were $10.3 million or $1.06 per diluted share compared to $10.1 million or $1.02 per diluted share for the nine-month period one year earlier. The increase in earnings for the nine-month period primarily related to a $2.9 million increase in net interest income, higher non-interest income of $0.1 million, lower non-interest expenses of $0.4 million, lower income taxes of $0.2 million, partially offset by a $3.5 million increase in the provision for credit losses.
•Pre-provision net revenue (“PPNR”) increased 21.1% during the quarter ended September 30, 2026, to $6.74 million from $5.56 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and increased 29.9% from $5.19 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•PPNR for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, increased 23.6% to $17.7 million from $14.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, largely due to increases in net interest income.
•Net interest income increased $1.0 million to $14.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, from $13.5 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and increased from $13.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase in net interest income from the second quarter of 2026 was primarily due to a 15 basis point increase in net interest margin, primarily due to a 17 basis point increase in loan yields from the prior quarter due to the impact of loans repricing and new loan production. In addition, the impact of a larger average balance of net interest earning assets increased net interest income by $0.2 million and the impact of one more day in the quarter added $0.1 million.
•The provision for credit losses was $0.13 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to a provision for credit losses of $4.33 million for the second quarter of 2026. Factors affecting the third quarter provision for credit losses relative to the second quarter included lower specific reserves and charge-offs on nonaccrual loans, lower required ACL provisions based on changes in loan characteristics, offset by the impact of new loan growth.
•Non-interest income increased by $0.3 million in the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, to $2.9 million from $2.6 million in the prior quarter and decreased $0.1 million from $3.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in the third quarter of 2026 from the second quarter of 2026 was primarily due to higher gains on equity securities and higher loan servicing income. The decrease in the third quarter of 2026 from the third quarter of 2025 was primarily due to lower gains on the sale of loans, partially offset by higher gains on equity securities, net.
•Non-interest expense increased $0.1 million to $10.6 million from $10.5 million for the previous quarter and decreased $0.4 million from $11.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in non-interest expense compared to the linked quarter was driven primarily by higher compensation costs, as third quarter incentive accruals reflected higher performance. Additionally, a one-time $0.1 million increase in data processing expense was more than offset by the decrease in other expenses, primarily related to a decrease in expenses related to credit quality. Non-interest expense in the third quarter of 2026 decreased $0.4 million from the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to data processing expenses. Amortization of intangible assets will decline in the fourth quarter as the remaining intangible will be fully amortized during the fourth quarter.
•The effective tax rate was 18.2% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to 10.9% for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and 18.8% for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase in the effective tax rate in the third quarter of 2026 from the second quarter of 2026 was due to the effective tax rate normalizing to historical levels after the extremely low effective rate in the second quarter of 2026 primarily due to the impact of lower net income in the second quarter.

The increase in loans reflected new commercial real estate and multi-family loan originations partially offset by reductions in C&I, agricultural and residential loans.
•Nonperforming assets decreased modestly by $0.2 million to $32.2 million at September 30, 2026, compared to $32.4 million at June 30, 2026. About the Company
Citizens Community Bancorp, Inc. (Nasdaq: “CZWI”) is the holding company of the Bank, a national bank based in Altoona, Wisconsin, currently serving customers primarily in Wisconsin and Minnesota through 21 branch locations. Its primary markets include the Chippewa Valley Region in Wisconsin, the Twin Cities and Mankato markets in Minnesota, and various rural communities around these areas. The Bank offers traditional community banking services to businesses, ag operators and consumers, including residential mortgage loans.
