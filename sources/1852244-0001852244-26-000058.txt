GXO Announces 2030 Financial Targets at Investor Day
GREENWICH, Conn., November 16, 2026 – Today, GXO Logistics, Inc. (NYSE: GXO), the world’s largest pure-play contract logistics provider, will host its 2026 Investor Day at the New York Stock Exchange. CEO Patrick Kelleher and members of GXO’s executive leadership team will present the Company’s strategy for value creation, its capital allocation framework and 2030 financial targets.
Patrick Kelleher, chief executive officer of GXO, said, “Our ambition is to lead the future of supply chain services in the next era of intelligent, AI-powered logistics and solve increasingly complex challenges for customers. Today’s Investor Day is an important milestone in our journey. Over the past year, GXO has materially evolved our operating model and commercial strategy. We’re building a stronger, more diversified business, while strengthening our position as the industry leader in technology, automation and AI.
“We have a significant opportunity ahead of us and a clear playbook to capture it. The multi-year financial targets we are introducing today reflect our confidence in driving durable, compounding shareholder value through the end of the decade. We know what we need to do. We have the team and capabilities to execute it. And we are energized about the opportunity ahead.”
GXO’s 2030 Financial Targets
•Greater than 7% organic revenue CAGR1;
•Greater than 6.5% adjusted EBITA margin; and
•40% to 50% free cash flow conversion rate on adjusted EBITDA.
Preliminary 2027 Outlook
The company’s initial full-year 2027 financial outlook is as follows:
•Above 5.5% organic revenue growth;
•Greater than 7.1% adjusted EBITDA margin; and
•Above 4.5% adjusted EBITA margin.
GXO’s Investor Day will take place today at The New York Stock Exchange and stream online via webcast at 9:00 am ET. Webcast and presentation materials will be available on the Company’s Investor Relations website at investors.gxo.com. A replay will be available following the event.
1 CAGR represents compound annual organic growth rate for the four-year period from fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year 2030.
About GXO
GXO Logistics, Inc. (NYSE: GXO) is the world’s largest pure-play contract logistics provider and is positioned to capitalize on the rapid growth of ecommerce, automation and outsourcing. GXO has over 150,000 team members across more than 1,000 facilities, totaling more than 200 million square feet. The company serves the world’s leading blue-chip companies to solve complex logistics challenges with technologically advanced supply chain and ecommerce solutions, at scale and with speed. GXO corporate headquarters is in Greenwich, Connecticut. Visit GXO.com for more information and connect with GXO on LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube.
Non-GAAP Financial Measures
As required by the rules of the Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”), this press release contains forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures. As discussed below, reconciliations of these measures to the most directly comparable measure under GAAP are not available without unreasonable effort.
GXO’s non-GAAP financial measures in this press release include: adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization (“adjusted EBITDA”), adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted earnings before interest, taxes and amortization (“adjusted EBITA”), adjusted EBITA margin, free cash flow, free cash flow conversion, organic revenue, and organic revenue growth.
We believe that the above adjusted financial measures facilitate analysis of our ongoing business operations because they exclude items that may not be reflective of, or are unrelated to, GXO’s core operating performance, and may assist investors with comparisons to prior periods and assessing trends in our underlying businesses. Other companies may calculate these non-GAAP financial measures differently, and therefore our measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies. GXO’s non-GAAP financial measures should only be used as supplemental measures of our operating performance.
We believe that adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted EBITA, and adjusted EBITA margin, improve comparability from period to period by removing the impact of our capital structure (interest expense), asset base (depreciation and amortization), tax impacts and other adjustments, which management has determined are not reflective of core operating activities and thereby assist investors with assessing trends in our underlying businesses.
We believe that organic revenue and organic revenue growth are important measures because they exclude the impact of foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations.
We believe that free cash flow and free cash flow conversion are important measures of our ability to repay maturing debt or fund other uses of capital that we believe will enhance stockholder value. We calculate free cash flow as cash flows from operations less capital expenditures plus proceeds from sale of property and equipment. We calculate free cash flow conversion as free cash flow divided by adjusted EBITDA, expressed as a percentage.
Management uses these non-GAAP financial measures in making financial, operating and planning decisions and evaluating GXO’s ongoing performance.
