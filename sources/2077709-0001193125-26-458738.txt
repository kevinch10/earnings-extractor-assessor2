ROC Delivers Record Quarterly Revenue of $8.0 Million in the Third-Quarter 2026, Up 50% YoY; Achieves Quarterly Profitability
Product revenue grew 24% to $5.7 million, driven by strong software licensing performance; gross margin reached 90% for the second consecutive quarter
Measurable results validate ROC’s core growth drivers; ROC ABIS grew 654% YoY to $2.2M and ROC SDK grew 52% YoY to $2.6M
Secured transformational federal beachhead contracts for ROC ABIS and ROC Evidence, including a $64.3 million Department of Justice digital evidence contract award, the largest in Company history
DENVER, Nov. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Rank One Computing Corporation d/b/a ROC (Nasdaq: ROC) (“ROC” or the “Company”), a U.S. leader in Vision AI, building unified biometric, video analytics, and digital evidence solutions, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“The third quarter marked a transformational period for ROC, highlighted by record revenue, a profitable quarter, and significant execution against the strategic objectives we established at our IPO,” said B. Scott Swann, ROC’s Chief Executive Officer. “We delivered record third-quarter revenue of $8.0 million, increasing 50% year over year and 57% sequentially, and our software licensing revenue drove a second consecutive quarter of 90% gross margin. Our strong results were driven by meaningful federal traction for ROC ABIS and expanded activity under an existing government R&D program, demonstrating the strategic investments we have made to execute our growth strategy are translating into measurable financial performance.”
“Importantly, we delivered on our commitment by establishing two foundational federal beachhead contracts for ROC ABIS and ROC Evidence, including the largest award in ROC’s history, a $64.3 million contract with the U.S. Department of Justice. These wins secure ROC’s position as a competitor in the Vision AI space. Our differentiation is validated in the accuracy, speed and efficiency of our technologies, supported by a highly specialized team that drives our unique value proposition. With these strategic federal programs in place, ROC is well-positioned with an active federal pipeline to pursue larger, longer-duration opportunities that generate durable, high-margin revenue.”
Mr. Swann continued, “We entered the fourth quarter from a position of strength, focused on disciplined execution, specifically addressing our three core growth products, ROC ABIS, ROC Evidence and ROC Watch. With the integration of ZTC strengthening ROC Evidence and a broad pipeline of opportunities across our commercial and federal markets, we believe we are building a foundation for sustained, long-term growth. We consider the third quarter an inflection point for ROC, as we secured two landmark contracts and reached record revenue. Achieving these significant milestones leaves us confident in a strong close to 2026.”
Third Quarter 2026 Business Updates
•
Secured a U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) ROC Evidence contract, the largest award in ROC’s history, with a total potential value of $64.3 million over eight years, including approximately $7.4 million under the initial one-year base period.
•
Secured ROC’s first strategic federal ROC ABIS contract, with a total potential value of $5.1 million over five years, including approximately $2.5 million in committed contract value, delivering this significant federal ABIS platform license during the quarter.
•
Continued strong commercial adoption of ROC SDK, generating $2.6 million in quarterly revenue, representing 52% year-over-year growth.
•
Expanded ROC Watch’s market presence with new government and commercial customer wins during the third quarter, reflecting continued adoption of ROC’s video analytics solutions.
Third Quarter 2026 Corporate Highlights and Subsequent Events
•
Completed the acquisition of Zuccaro Technical Consulting (ZTC) on August 31, 2026, expanding ROC Evidence with proprietary digital forensics capabilities, experienced technical personnel, and established federal customer relationships.
•
Achieved #1 global accuracy rankings across three National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Face Analysis Technology Evaluation (FATE) Age Estimation and Verification benchmarks, including Visa, Application, and Mugshot datasets.
•
Appointed Dr. Anil K. Jain, a globally recognized pioneer in biometrics, pattern recognition, and computer vision, to ROC’s Board of Directors.
•
Launched ROC's first SaaS product, an Age Estimation API that establishes a foundation for new recurring revenue opportunities across the Company’s Vision AI portfolio.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results (as compared to Third Quarter 2025)
Revenue reached a record $8.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of approximately $2.7 million, or 50%, compared to $5.3 million in the prior-year period. Growth was driven by higher product revenue, particularly ROC ABIS and ROC SDK, and increased government R&D contract revenue from expanded activity under a U.S. government research program that commenced in the second quarter of 2026.
Product revenue was $5.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of approximately $1.1 million, or 24%, compared to $4.6 million in the prior year period. The increase was driven primarily by significant growth in ROC ABIS and continued ROC SDK expansion, partially offset by lower ROC Watch revenue following the completion of a significant government deployment in the fourth quarter of 2025.
During the third quarter of 2026, ROC's product revenue performance reflected continued commercial adoption and expansion across its Vision AI portfolio:
•
ROC SDK revenue was $2.6 million, a YoY increase of 52% from $1.7 million, reflecting new customer contracts and expanded commercial adoption.
•
ROC ABIS revenue was $2.2 million, a YoY increase of 654% from $287,000, advancing commercialization with a significant federal ABIS platform license delivered during the quarter.
•
ROC Watch revenue was $0.8 million, compared to $2.5 million in the prior year quarter, due to a mission deployment that was completed in Q4 2025. Sequentially, ROC Watch revenue increased 219% from $0.2 million in the second quarter of 2026.
•
ROC Evidence revenue was approximately $63,000, following initial commercialization in the second quarter 2026. The DOJ contract base-year award contributed revenue for initial services delivered in the third quarter of 2026 and will contribute more meaningful revenue in the fourth quarter of 2026 and continuing through the third quarter of 2027.
•
ROC Enroll revenue was approximately $59,000, a YoY increase of 55% from $38,000.
Government R&D contract revenue was $2.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of approximately $1.4 million, or 175%, year over year. The increase was driven by expanded activity under a U.S. Government research and development program that commenced in the second quarter of 2026.
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company began reporting Service Contracts revenue, which includes revenue from pure service contracts that do not include a software license, hardware, or R&D deliverable. Service Contracts revenue was approximately $0.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, primarily attributable to revenue recognized from the ZTC acquisition on August 31, 2026.
Gross profit was $7.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of approximately $3.0 million or 71%, compared to $4.2 million in the prior-year period. Gross margin improved to 90% from 79% in the prior-year period, primarily reflecting a favorable revenue mix driven by higher-margin software licensing revenue, including a significant federal ROC ABIS platform license delivered during the quarter.
Gross margin may fluctuate between reporting periods based on the timing and mix of software license revenue, services, hardware deployments, and government R&D contract activity.
Operating expenses totaled $6.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $4.3 million in the prior-year period. The increase primarily reflected planned investments in personnel, operations, infrastructure, and the continued development of ROC's Vision AI capabilities, as well as costs associated with the ZTC acquisition.
Net income was approximately $0.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared with a net loss of approximately $0.8 million in the prior-year period. Basic and diluted earnings per share were $0.03, compared with a basic and diluted loss per share of $0.05 in the prior-year period. ROC’s quarterly profitability reflects a favorable revenue mix shift toward software licensing and the structural benefits inherent in ROC's technology platform.
As of September 30, 2026, ROC had $7.5 million in cash, approximately $14.8 million in working capital, and no borrowings under its revolving line of credit.
Accounts receivable increased to $11.1 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $5.6 million as of June 30, 2026, primarily reflecting higher revenue and the timing of customer invoicing under contractual billing arrangements.
The Company completed its acquisition of ZTC on August 31, 2026, as previously announced in a Current Report on Form 8-K filed on September 2, 2026 (the “ZTC 8-K”). ZTC’s operating results for the post-acquisition period of September 1, 2026 through September 30, 2026 have been included in ROC’s consolidated financial statements contained in its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the period ended September 30, 2026, which was filed on November 12, 2026. In addition, (A) ZTC’s audited financial statements as of December 31, 2025 and 2024 and for the years then ended, together with the related independent auditor’s report; (B) ZTC’s unaudited interim financial statements as of June 30, 2026 and for the six months ended June 30, 2026; (C) the unaudited pro forma condensed combined balance sheet of ROC as of June 30, 2026, giving effect to the ZTC transaction as if it had occurred on June 30, 2026; and (D) the unaudited pro forma condensed combined statements of operations of ROC for the year ended December 31, 2025 and the six months ended June 30, 2026, in each case giving effect to the ZTC transaction as if it had occurred on January 1, 2025 have been prepared in accordance with Article 11 of Regulation S-X and filed separately under a Form 8-K/A filing dated November 12, 2026, which amends the ZTC 8-K as required under applicable SEC regulations and in accordance with those requirements.

About ROC
ROC is a leading U.S. developer of Vision AI, delivering sovereign biometrics, video analytics, and digital evidence through a unified platform. This enables agency and integrator partners to unlock faster, more accurate, and cost-efficient capabilities. At its core, ROC transforms raw pixels into real-time operational awareness for defense, public safety, and digital commerce. The Company is headquartered in Denver, Colo., with additional hubs in Grand Rapids, Mich., and Morgantown, W. Va. For more information, please visit the Company’s website: www.roc.ai.
