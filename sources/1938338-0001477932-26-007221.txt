Globaltech Reports Third Quarter 2026 Revenue of $10.3 Million, Up 86% Year Over Year, and Operating Income of $1.4 Million
Nine-month revenue reaches $31.9 million; the third quarter is the Company's first quarter of operating income in 2026
• | A profitable quarter. Net income of $0.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared with a net loss of $0.7 million a year earlier, and Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP, reconciled below) of $3.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026.
• | Our technology business is growing. Technology services, the line closest to Globaltech’s AI, data and software strategy, grew revenue 37% in the quarter and 57% for the nine months, year over year.
• | A broader revenue base. About 81% of third-quarter 2026 net revenue came from the United Kingdom and the United Arab Emirates; a year earlier, all of it came from Pakistan.
• | Progress toward Nasdaq. OTCQB quotation, completion of 1-for-3 reverse stock split, independent board committees and current SEC reporting are complete; the listing application remains under review.
RENO, Nevada, November 23, 2026 - Globaltech Corporation (OTCQB: GLTK) (“Globaltech” or the “Company”), a platform technology company with telecommunications, retail, AI and big data technology-services operations, today discussed its results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. The Company filed its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on November 13, 2026.
Results include 123 Investments Limited, which operates the Moda in Pelle footwear business. Globaltech acquired a 51% interest in 123 Investments Limited on December 15, 2025, and its results were not included in the comparable 2025 periods. Most of the year-over-year increase in revenue reflects that acquisition.
All share and per-share amounts in this release reflect the 1-for-3 reverse stock split that became effective on August 27, 2026.
Third-Quarter Highlights
• | Net revenue increased 86% to $10.3 million, compared with $5.5 million for the third quarter of 2025.
• | Operating income was $1.4 million, compared with an operating loss of $0.5 million in the prior-year quarter.
• | Consolidated net income was $0.7 million, compared with a consolidated net loss of $0.7 million in the prior-year quarter. Net income attributable to Globaltech common shareholders was $0.3 million, or $0.006 per basic and diluted share. The Company's Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q attributes the change from a net loss to net income mainly to the inclusion of 123 Investments Limited.
• | Third-quarter net income did not rely on the disposal and investment gains that contributed to second-quarter results. Other income (loss), net, was a loss of $0.1 million.
• | Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, was $3.0 million, compared with $10,293 in the prior-year quarter. A reconciliation to net income appears below.

Nine-Month Highlights
• | Net revenue increased 105% to $31.9 million, compared with $15.5 million in the prior-year period.
• | Operating loss was $5.7 million, compared with $2.2 million in the prior-year period. Nine Month results included approximately $2.8 million of non-cash share-based consultancy expense.
• | Consolidated net loss was $2.8 million, compared with $3.0 million in the prior-year period. Net loss attributable to Globaltech common shareholders was $1.6 million, or $0.031 per share, compared with $1.6 million, or $0.033 per share during the same period last year.
• | The nine-month net loss is after other income of $5.1 million, most of it recorded in the second quarter: a gain of approximately $2.9 million on the disposal of two 123 Investments Limited subsidiaries, which was substantially non-cash, and a gain of approximately $1.8 million on the sale of an investment.
• | Net cash provided by operating activities was $2.1 million, compared with $0.7 million in the prior-year period.
• | Adjusted EBITDA was $3.5 million, compared with $0.1 million in the prior-year period. Nine-month Adjusted EBITDA includes the second-quarter gains described above and is after the non-cash consultancy expense. A reconciliation to net income appears below.

Nasdaq Uplisting Update
Globaltech's application to list its common stock on the Nasdaq Capital Market remains under review by Nasdaq and such application may not be approved on a timely basis, if at all. The Company's common stock continues to trade on the OTCQB Market under the symbol “GLTK”.
Steps completed to date in preparation for the planned listing:
• | OTCQB. The Company's common stock was approved for quotation on the OTCQB Venture Market on March 26, 2026.
• | Reverse stock split. The 1-for-3 reverse stock split of our outstanding common stock became effective on August 27, 2026. It was intended to support the Company's efforts to satisfy Nasdaq's minimum share-price requirement and does not by itself establish compliance with all listing standards. Trading resumed under the symbol “GLTK” on September 28, 2026.
• | Governance. The Board's Audit, Compensation, and Nominating and Corporate Governance Committees operate under charters adopted effective January 7, 2026 and are composed of directors the Board has determined to be independent under Nasdaq rules, as the Company confirmed on October 5, 2026.
• | Reporting. The Company filed amended annual and quarterly reports on October 21, 2026 and its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the third quarter on November 13, 2026.
Work that remains to potentially achieve the Nasdaq listing.
The Company must satisfy all applicable initial listing standards and obtain Nasdaq's approval. Completion remains subject to market conditions and financing requirements. As stated in its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q, the Company may seek to raise equity through private or public offerings in connection with the uplisting, and any sale of equity would dilute existing stockholders.
On September 17, 2026, the Company said management remained hopeful of completing the uplisting in the fourth quarter of 2026. Management continues to work toward that timing. There can be no assurance regarding the timing or completion of the uplisting.
If Nasdaq approves the application, the Company will announce such approval through a Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the SEC and a press release.

Non-GAAP financial measure. Adjusted EBITDA is not a measure under U.S. generally accepted accounting principles. The Company defines it as net income (loss) plus depreciation and amortization, finance cost, income taxes and exchange (gain) loss. The Company has included Adjusted EBITDA as a supplement to Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) measures of performance to provide investors with an additional financial analytical framework which management uses, in addition to historical operating results, as the basis for financial, operational and planning decisions and present measurements that third parties have indicated are useful in assessing the Company and its results of operations. The most directly comparable GAAP measure to Adjusted EBITDA is net loss. Adjusted EBITDA is presented because we believe it provides additional useful information to investors by excluding the impact of non-operating, non-recurring, and certain non-cash items, as well as items such as interest, taxes, and depreciation and amortization, thereby facilitating comparisons of operating performance across periods. Adjusted EBITDA is also frequently used by analysts, investors and other interested parties to evaluate companies in our industry. Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool, and you should not consider it in isolation, or as a substitute for analysis of our operating results as reported under GAAP. Some of these limitations are: Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect cash expenditures, future requirements for capital expenditures, or contractual commitments; Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect changes in, or cash requirements for, working capital needs; and Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect the significant interest expense, or the cash requirements necessary to service interest or principal payments, on debt or cash income tax payments. For example, although depreciation and amortization are noncash charges, the assets being depreciated and amortized will often have to be replaced in the future, and Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect any cash requirements for such replacements. Additionally, other companies in our industry may calculate Adjusted EBITDA differently than the Company does, limiting its usefulness as a comparative measure. You should not consider Adjusted EBITDA in isolation, or as a substitute for analysis of the Company’s results as reported under GAAP. The Company’s presentation of these measures should not be construed as an inference that future results will be unaffected by unusual or nonrecurring items. We compensate for these limitations by providing a reconciliation of this non-GAAP measure to the most comparable GAAP measure (above). We encourage investors and others to review our business, results of operations, and financial information in their entirety, not to rely on any single financial measure, and to view this non-GAAP measure in conjunction with the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure. The Company presents the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure with equal or greater prominence than the non-GAAP measures.

Restatement of Prior Periods. As disclosed in the Company's Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on October 2, 2026, the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors of the Company concluded on September 29, 2026 that the Company's financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2025 and for the quarters ended March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2026 should no longer be relied upon. The cause was an error in assessing whether deferred tax assets of the Company’s majority owned subsidiary Worldcall Telecom Limited could be realized as of December 31, 2025.
The Company recorded an additional valuation allowance of approximately $8.4 million. This increased 2025 income tax expense and net loss and reduced total shareholders' equity at December 31, 2025 by the same amount. The Company filed amended reports on Form 10-K/A and Form 10-Q/A on October 21, 2026. The correction did not affect the Company's previously reported revenue, cash position or cash flows. December 31, 2025 balance sheet amounts in this release are as restated.
The determination was made by the Company's Audit Committee following discussions with management and the Company's independent registered public accounting firm. The Audit Committee operates under a charter adopted in January 2026.
In connection with the restatement, management identified material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting, including one related to this error, and concluded that the Company's disclosure controls and procedures were not effective as of September 30, 2026. As described in its amended Annual Report on Form 10-K/A, the Company is adopting additional internal control mechanisms and is engaging an outside consultant to assist. The material weaknesses have not yet been remediated. The Audit Committee oversees the Company's financial reporting and management's remediation work.
